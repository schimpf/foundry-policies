## About

See what fills your phone and free up space, fully offline.

Storage Analyzer does not ask for the internet permission, so Android does not let it go online at all. No ads, no analytics, no crash reports, no account, no cloud. Everything it finds stays on your phone. You can check this yourself: the app shows its network permissions, read live from Android, on its privacy screen.

## Features

### What it finds

- Large files, sorted by size, with filters for images, videos, audio, documents, APKs and archives.
- Exact duplicates: files over 1 MB with the same size are compared by SHA-256 checksum, so only true copies are shown. The newest copy is kept by default, and at least one copy is always kept.
- Old files not changed for more than 90 days, grouped into Downloads, Screenshots, WhatsApp media, APK installers and other files.
- Folder sizes as a map you can tap through, with a list view and breadcrumb to go back.
- Space used by each app (app, data and cache), with a shortcut to the app's system settings to clear its cache.

### Safe delete

Deleted files go to the app's Trash first. Undo right away, or restore them from Trash for 7 days. After that they are deleted for good. Files are moved, not copied, so it is fast even for big videos. Folders are never deleted.

### You stay in control

- Scans run only when you tap Scan, never in the background.
- Full mode uses All files access to check every folder on internal storage and SD card.
- Prefer not to grant that? Limited mode only looks at the photos, videos and music Android shares with apps.
- Usage access is only asked for on the Apps screen, and only app sizes are read.

### Also

Note: uninstalling the app also empties its Trash. Restore files you want to keep first.

- Home screen widget with used storage, space you can free and last scan time.
- Light and dark theme from your system setting.
- Phone and tablet layouts.
- Works with screen readers.

## Questions and answers

### Does the app go online or share my data?

No. The app does not request the internet permission, so Android does not let it connect at all. It has no ads, analytics, crash reports or account, and its privacy screen shows the network permissions read live from Android.

### What does the app find, and what does it not do?

It finds large files, exact duplicates (same SHA-256 checksum), files not changed for more than 90 days, folder sizes and the space each app uses. It scans only when you tap Scan, never in the background, and it does not match similar photos or clear the cache of other apps for you.

### Are deleted files gone right away?

No. Deleted files go to the app's Trash, where you can undo or restore them for 7 days before they are deleted for good. Folders are never deleted, and uninstalling the app empties its Trash.

### Do I have to give access to all files?

No. Full mode uses All files access to check every folder on internal storage and the SD card. Limited mode only looks at the photos, videos and music that Android shares with apps.

### Which Android versions does it run on?

Android 10 and newer. Phone and tablet layouts, a light and dark theme and a home screen widget are included.

### Do I have to pay for anything besides the app?

No. The app is a one-time purchase on Google Play, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads. The app is available in English only.

Privacy Policy

Ad-Free Storage Analyzer (es.schimpf.adfreestorageanalyzer)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf · Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective date: 9 November 2026

Short version: the app does not collect, send or share any data. It has no internet permission, so Android does not let it go online. Everything it finds stays on your device.

1. No network access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission (or network state permissions). Without it, Android blocks all network access for the app. It contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account, no cloud backup and no in-app purchases. You can check this in the app under Settings › No network access, or on Google Play under "About this app › App permissions".

2. What the app reads on your device

When you start a scan, the app reads your storage to measure it:

  • Folder and file names, paths, sizes, file types and last-modified dates on internal storage and SD card.
  • The content of files over 1 MB that have the same size as another file, only to compute a SHA-256 checksum and find exact duplicates. File contents are not stored or looked at in any other way.
  • On the Apps screen, if you grant Usage access: the app, data and cache size of each installed app you can launch. Which apps you use, and when, is not read or stored.

Scans only run when you start them, while the app is open.

3. What the app stores, and where

  • Latest scan results (folder sizes, paths and details of large, old and duplicate files, checksums, volume totals) in a database in the app's private storage. A new scan replaces the old one.
  • Settings (whether setup is done, scan mode) in the app's private storage.
  • Trash: files you delete are moved into a trash folder on the same storage volume, inside the app's own folder, with their original path. They are deleted for good after 7 days, when you empty Trash, or when you uninstall the app.
  • Home screen widget: shows used storage, the size that can be freed and the last scan time. It shows no file names.

The app turns off Android backup and device-to-device transfer, so this data is not copied to Google Drive or another phone. File paths are not written to logs.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy
All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE); on Android 10, storage read/write To measure every folder, find large, old and duplicate files, and move files you choose to Trash. Optional: you can use limited mode instead.
Photos, videos, music (READ_MEDIA_* / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) Limited mode only: to measure the media files Android shares with apps.
Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) Optional, asked only on the Apps screen: Android only gives app sizes to apps with this access. Only sizes are read.
Run at startup, keep awake (added automatically by Android's WorkManager) A daily job that deletes Trash items older than 7 days. It does not scan.

You can turn off any of these in Android's system settings at any time.

5. Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, including the developer. The developer never receives any data from the app.

6. Google Play

The app is sold on Google Play. Purchases, downloads and any reviews are handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer receives only the usual anonymous sales and install reports from Google Play.

7. Deleting your data

All app data lives on your device. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Uninstalling also deletes everything in the app's Trash, so restore files you want to keep first.

8. Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Math Alarm Problems: Easy, Medium and Hard Examples

A math alarm will not switch off until you solve a sum. This page shows what the three difficulty levels in Alarm Clock look like, how each problem is built, and how to choose a level.

How the three levels work

Each level uses a fixed pattern. The numbers inside the pattern are picked at random.

LevelPatternNumber rangesAnswer range
Easya + ba and b are 1 to 92 to 18
Mediuma × b + ca is 10 to 99, b is 2 to 9, c is 1 to 9921 to 990
Harda × b − ca is 10 to 99, b is 10 to 99, c is 1 to 991 to 9,800

The hard level never gives a negative answer, because the smallest product is 10 × 10 = 100 and c is at most 99.

Worked examples

Easy: a + b

7 + 8

  1. Add the two digits: 7 + 8 = 15.
  2. Type 15.

Medium: a × b + c

47 × 6 + 35

  1. Split the big number: 47 × 6 = 40 × 6 + 7 × 6 = 240 + 42 = 282.
  2. Add the last number: 282 + 35 = 317.
  3. Type 317.

Hard: a × b − c

68 × 43 − 52

  1. Split 43 into 40 and 3.
  2. 68 × 40 = 2,720 and 68 × 3 = 204.
  3. Add them: 2,720 + 204 = 2,924.
  4. Subtract the last number: 2,924 − 52 = 2,872.
  5. Type 2,872.

Mental math tricks that help

  • Split the multiplier. Break a two-digit number into tens and ones, multiply each part, then add.
  • Round and fix. 49 × 6 is 50 × 6 − 6 = 294.
  • Do the multiply first. In a × b + c and a × b − c, the product comes first and c comes last.
  • Write it down. Holding numbers in your head is the hard part when you are half asleep. A pen next to the bed turns it into a writing task.

Which level should you pick?

  • Easy takes a few seconds. It makes you look at the screen and use your fingers, but it is a light hurdle.
  • Medium takes around half a minute for most people and needs real attention. It is a sensible default.
  • Hard needs two-digit multiplication. Expect a minute or more, and expect to be fully awake afterward.

You can also change how many problems you must solve, from 1 to 10. The default is 3 problems at medium level. Three medium problems in a row are hard to do with your eyes closed.

What happens when you get it wrong

  • You cannot skip the mission.
  • After 3 failed attempts, Alarm Clock offers an easy problem (the a + b type) so a half-asleep mistake never traps you.
  • If you leave the app or turn the screen off during the mission, the alarm rings again after one minute.
  • Snooze is not possible once the mission has started, so finish it first.

Set up a math alarm

  1. Open the alarm editor and set the time and days.
  2. Pick Math as the mission.
  3. Choose the difficulty and the number of problems.
  4. Save the alarm.

When the alarm rings, tap Dismiss. The first problem appears, and the alarm keeps ringing until you have solved them all.

Why this app

Alarm Clock is made for people who sleep through normal alarms. The volume rises over a time you choose, the alarm uses the alarm volume so it rings on silent, and a setup guide helps the alarm survive phone battery savers. It has no internet permission and no account, and it is open source under the GNU GPL v3 or later. Other missions are available too: shaking, scanning a QR code or barcode, and walking a set number of steps.

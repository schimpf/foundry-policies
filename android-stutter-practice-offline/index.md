## About

Practise fluent speech on your own, anywhere, without an account or an internet connection.

Stutter Practice is a toolkit for adults who stutter. It brings together exercises that many speech therapists use, in one calm, private app.

## Features

### Delayed auditory feedback (DAF)

Hear your own voice a moment later through wired or USB headphones. Set the delay from 25 to 250 ms with a slider, plus and minus buttons or quick presets. A short calibration reads your phone's own audio delay, and you can fine-tune it by ear, so the delay you choose is close to the delay you hear. The app warns you when Bluetooth or the speaker is active, because they add extra delay.

### Paced speech

A visual metronome sets a target rate from 40 to 200 syllables per minute. Practice sentences light up one syllable per beat, with the next sentence shown below. Turn on vibration to feel the beat without looking.

### Easy onset

Start words gently. A live onset meter shows how softly your voice rises and tells you whether the start was gentle or too sudden. 12 exercises go from breath and sighs to full sentences.

### Read aloud

30 English texts at three levels: everyday talk, phone calls, work and stories. Each word is highlighted at your chosen pace. You can combine reading with DAF.

### Practice log and widget

Every session is saved on your phone with its settings. See your streak, today's minutes and a weekly chart. Add the home-screen widget to keep your streak in view.

### Private by design

- No internet permission. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Your voice is processed live and never recorded or stored.
- No account, no ads, no analytics.
- Your practice log stays on your phone unless you switch on Google backup.

### Free, with a one-time unlock

Free: DAF, paced speech, 3 easy-onset exercises, 3 texts, the full practice log and the widget. One payment unlocks all 12 exercises, all 30 texts and DAF while reading. No subscription.

### Accessible

Light and dark theme follow your system. Works on phones and tablets. Large touch targets and screen reader labels throughout.

Use wired or USB headphones for DAF. Texts are in English.

Stutter Practice is not a medical device and does not replace therapy. It works well alongside guidance from a speech and language therapist.

## Questions and answers

### What can I practice with the app?

Delayed auditory feedback (DAF) with a delay from 25 to 250 ms, paced speech with a metronome from 40 to 200 syllables per minute, easy onset with a live onset meter, and read-aloud with 30 English texts. It also keeps a practice log with streaks and a home-screen widget.

### Does it replace speech therapy?

No. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat anything. It works as practice alongside a speech and language therapist.

### Where does my data stay, and is my voice recorded?

Your voice is processed live and never recorded or stored. Sessions, settings and calibration stay on your phone, and the app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics. The log is only included in Google backup if you switch that on.

### Is the app free, or do I have to buy something?

DAF, paced speech, 3 easy-onset exercises, 3 texts, the full practice log and the widget are free. One one-time purchase unlocks all 12 exercises, all 30 texts and DAF while reading. There is no subscription.

### Which Android versions and headphones does it need?

It runs on Android 8.1 or newer, on phones and tablets. For DAF use wired or USB headphones, because Bluetooth and the phone speaker add extra delay and the app shows a warning when either is active.

### Which language are the exercises in?

The app and its practice texts are in English. The website pages are also available in German and Spanish.

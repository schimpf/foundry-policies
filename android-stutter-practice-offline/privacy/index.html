Privacy Policy

Stutter Practice (Android Stutter Practice Offline, package es.schimpf.androidstutterpracticeoffline)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf · Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective date: 9 November 2026

Summary

  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything to the developer or anyone else.
  • Your voice is processed live on your device and is never recorded or stored.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Your practice log and settings stay on your device unless you switch on Google backup in the app.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access for delayed auditory feedback (DAF), the easy-onset meter and DAF while reading. Audio is processed in memory while a practice screen is open and is played back to your headphones with a delay or turned into a loudness level. It is never written to storage, logs or reports, and it never leaves your device. The microphone stops when you leave the practice screen or the app goes to the background.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Practice sessions: start time, time zone, duration, practice mode and the settings used (for example delay, target rate, text or exercise).
  • Settings: delays and rates you chose, whether first-run setup is done, the audio latency calibration (a number of milliseconds and the type and name of the audio output, for example "Wired headphones"), and whether Google backup is on.
  • Unlock state: whether the one-time unlock is active and a one-way SHA-256 hash of the Google Play purchase token. The token itself is not stored.

Streaks and the home-screen widget are calculated from your sessions on the device. The developer has no access to any of this data.

Google backup (off by default)

By default nothing from the app is included in Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer. If you turn on "Include in Google backup" in Settings, Android may include the data listed above in your Google account backup, handled by Google under its own privacy policy. You can turn it off again at any time.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details. The app talks to the Google Play Store app on your device to start a purchase and to check whether you own the unlock. As with any Play purchase, Google provides the developer with standard order records (such as order number, date, product and country) in the Play Console. The developer does not combine these with any other data. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Other permissions

  • Vibration: used only for the optional beat vibration in paced speech.
  • Billing: used only for the one-time unlock through Google Play.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, storage or any network permission.

Sharing

The app does not share or sell any data. There are no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

Deleting your data

All data is on your device. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android Settings › Apps › Stutter Practice › Storage, or uninstall the app. If you turned on Google backup, you can delete backups in your Google account or device backup settings.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Health note

Stutter Practice is a practice tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

## About

Every "no" protects a "yes". Anti-Bucket List is a private journal for your deliberate declines: the invitation you turned down, the upgrade you skipped, the extra project you didn't take on. Write down what you said no to and why, then check back later to see if you are still glad you did.

## Features

### Write it down in seconds

- What you declined and why, in your own words
- Reason tags: Time, Money, Energy, Values and Boundaries, plus tags of your own
- How saying no felt, on a scale from relieved to uneasy
- The date you decided
- Optional money saved and hours saved

### "Still glad?" check-ins

After 30 and 90 days, a reminder asks how you feel now: still glad, unsure, or regret it. Add a short note if you like. Over time you see which kinds of no serve you well. If notifications are off, due check-ins also show at the top of your journal.

### Your journal

- Search the titles and reasons you wrote
- Filter by tag
- Filter by check-in result: pending, due, glad, unsure or regret
- Open any entry to see its full reason and its check-in history

### Stats

- Total no's, money saved and hours saved
- No's per tag
- Your check-in results as glad, unsure and regret

### Home-screen widget

See this month's no's and what you saved, and add a new entry with one tap.

### Backup and restore

Save your journal to a JSON file in a place you choose, and restore it later. The file is not encrypted, and the app warns you before every backup.

### Private by design

- No account and no sign-up
- No analytics and no advertising
- The app has no internet permission. Your journal stays on your phone.
- Excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer

### Pro features

Everything described above works without a purchase and has no limits. Pro is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. It adds:

Made for people working on boundaries, minimalism or better decisions, and for anyone who wants a record that saying no was the right call.

Works on phones and tablets, in light and dark mode, with screen reader support.

- App lock with fingerprint, face or device PIN. It also hides the widget numbers and notification text.
- Advanced stats: no's per month for the last 12 months, regret rate per tag, and money and hours saved per tag
- CSV export for spreadsheets

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It is a journal of the things you said no to. You write what you declined and why, add tags and how it felt, and after 30 and 90 days it asks "Still glad?".

### Does it need an account or internet?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no ads, and the app has no internet permission.

### Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. The journal is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. You can save it to a JSON file you choose and restore it later, but that file is not encrypted.

### What needs a purchase?

Entries, tags, check-ins, search, basic stats, the widget and backup are free and have no limits. Pro is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. It adds app lock, CSV export and advanced stats.

### Which Android versions does it support?

Android 8.0 or newer. It works on phones and tablets, in light and dark mode.

### Can I move my journal to a new phone?

Yes, with a backup. Save a JSON file on the old phone, copy it over, and restore it in Settings on the new one.

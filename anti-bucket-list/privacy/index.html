Anti-Bucket List: Privacy Policy

App: Anti-Bucket List (es.schimpf.antibucketlist)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

Short version

Anti-Bucket List is a private journal. The developer does not collect, receive, store or share any of your data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not have permission to use the internet.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your journal entries: title, reason, tags, how it felt, optional money and hours saved, and dates.
  • Your check-in answers (glad, unsure, regret) and optional notes.
  • Settings: whether app lock and reminders are on, and whether Pro is unlocked.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is protected by Android's own device encryption, not by extra encryption inside the app. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it does not move to a new phone by itself. Deleting an entry removes it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes everything.

Network use

The app does not declare the internet permission, so it cannot open network connections itself. There is no server behind the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only for local "Still glad you said no?" reminders after 30 and 90 days. They are created on your device. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android.
  • Google Play Billing: used for the optional Pro purchase (see below).
  • Biometrics / device lock: not a permission the app holds. If you turn on app lock (Pro), Android shows its own unlock prompt. The app only learns whether it succeeded. It never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.

Purchases

Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles payment and its own records under Google's privacy policy. The app only asks the Play Store on your device which purchases you own and keeps that result on your device. The app sends nothing about you or your purchase to the developer. As with any Play app, the developer can see sales reports that Google provides in Play Console.

Pro can also be unlocked with an unlock code. The code is checked on your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Files you create

Backup (JSON) and CSV export (Pro) are written only to a location you pick with the Android file picker. These files are not encrypted. Anyone who can open them can read your journal. The app warns you before every backup. Restoring a backup replaces everything in the app with the file you choose. Once you save a file, it is under your control and outside the app.

Widget and notifications

The home-screen widget shows this month's counts, money and hours saved. Notifications can show the title of an entry. Both are visible to anyone who can see your screen. When app lock is on, the widget shows "Locked" and notifications show a generic text instead.

Sharing

The developer does not share data with anyone, because the developer has none. The app contains no advertising or analytics code.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your rights

Because no personal data reaches the developer, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct or delete. You control your data in the app. If you have a question or believe something here is wrong, write to apps@schimpf.es.

Changes

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Check the date to see when the policy last changed.

## About

Beekeeping Hive Log is a simple inspection log for backyard beekeepers. It works without an account and keeps all your data on your device.

## Features

### Fast inspections, even with gloves

- Large buttons for queen seen, eggs, brood pattern and temperament
- Optional varroa count with a minus and plus stepper
- Answers from the last inspection are filled in, so a routine check takes a few taps

### Everything per hive

- One timeline per hive with inspections, treatments and feedings
- Varroa trend chart with a guideline line at 3 mites per day, plus a text summary and a table view
- Hive status at a glance: OK, needs attention, or in a withdrawal period
- Archive hives you no longer keep without losing their history

### Treatments and feeding

- Log treatments with a withdrawal period. The app shows a warning until it ends and can remind you on the end date. It never blocks you.
- Log feedings: syrup 1:1, syrup 2:1, fondant or pollen patty
- Metric or imperial units

### Reminders

- One-off reminders or repeats every N days
- Notifications at a time you choose, 08:00 by default
- Home screen widget with your next reminders and the hives that need attention

### Your data stays on your device

- The app has no internet permission and sends nothing anywhere
- No account, no analytics
- Backup and restore to a file you pick. The backup is plain JSON and not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

### For every screen

- Phones and tablets, with a list and detail view side by side on larger screens
- Light and dark theme follow your system setting
- Screen reader labels, large touch targets and high-contrast support

### Free and pro

Included without purchase: up to 3 active hives, all logging, reminders, the widget and backup. Archived hives do not count towards the 3. Pro is a single purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. It unlocks unlimited hives and CSV and PDF export that you can share with a vet or inspector.

The varroa guideline line is a display hint, not treatment advice. Follow local regulations and product labels for treatments and withdrawal periods.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It logs inspections, treatments and feedings for each hive on one timeline, and charts varroa counts. It also sets one-off or repeating reminders and has a home screen widget.

### What does it not do?

It has no accounts, cloud sync, photos, harvest logging or multiple apiaries. It does not give treatment advice, and the varroa line at 3 mites per day is only a display hint.

### Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. The app has no internet permission, no account and no analytics. Backup and restore use a plain JSON file you pick, which is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

### What is free and what needs a purchase?

Up to 3 active hives, all logging, reminders, the widget and backup are free, and archived hives do not count. Pro is a single purchase through Google Play, not a subscription, and adds unlimited hives and CSV and PDF export.

### Does it block me during a withdrawal period?

No. It shows a warning until the withdrawal ends and can offer a reminder for the end date, but you can always save.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer, on phones and tablets.

Privacy Policy: Beekeeping Hive Log

App: Beekeeping Hive Log (es.schimpf.beekeepinghivelog)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

Summary

The developer does not collect, receive or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app stores the data you type in: hive names and notes, inspections, treatments, feedings, reminders, and your settings (unit system, reminder time). This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device only. The developer has no access to it.

Network use

The app does not request the internet permission and cannot send data over the network. It has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set, at the time you choose. Reminders are created and shown on your device.
  • Receive boot completed: to schedule your reminders again after the device restarts or the app updates.
  • Google Play Billing: to let you buy the optional Pro upgrade. See below.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google and are never seen by the app or the developer. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase exists, and keeps the result on your device. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Backup, restore and export

  • Backup writes a plain JSON file to a location you pick with the system file picker. The file is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere private.
  • Restore reads a file you pick and replaces the data on your device.
  • CSV and PDF export (Pro) write a file to a location you pick. If you share the file, the receiving person or service handles it under their own rules.
  • The app does not copy data to Android cloud backup or device transfer.

Home screen widget

The optional widget shows your next reminders and the number of hives needing attention. It reads data from the app on your device only.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored by the app. Backup and export files you created stay where you saved them, and you can delete them yourself. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to delete on a server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

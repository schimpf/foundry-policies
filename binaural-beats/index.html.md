## About

Binaural Beats creates soft beat tones live on your phone. Pick a starting preset, adjust it to your taste and let it play in the background while you relax, meditate, work or fall asleep.

Four ways to hear a beat • Binaural: each ear gets a slightly different tone, and you hear the difference as a gentle pulse. Use headphones. • Monaural: both tones are mixed into both ears. Works on speakers too. • Isochronic: one tone that softly pulses on and off. • Bilateral: one tone moves calmly back and forth between your left and right ear. Switching modes crossfades smoothly, without clicks.

Shape the sound • Beat from 0.25 to 45 Hz, with a live label for the band (Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, Gamma). • Carrier tone from 100 to 1000 Hz. • Optional white, pink or brown noise, with its own volume. • Sine or soft square pulse for isochronic and bilateral mode.

Sessions that fade gently Choose 5 to 90 minutes or no limit. Every session fades in, fades out at the end, and pause, resume and stop fade softly too.

Journeys Build a session from steps that hold a beat or glide it slowly to another one, for example from 12 Hz down to 3 Hz to wind down in the evening. A chart shows how the beat changes over time.

Presets Eight starting points are included: Deep Sleep, Nap, Relax, Meditation, Calm Focus, Active Focus, Gamma Attention and Wind Down. Save your own versions, duplicate, rename and delete them, and import or export them as a file.

Plays in the background Playback keeps going with the screen off, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen. A home-screen widget shows the current preset with a play button. The app does not pause other audio, so you can play beats quietly under your music or a podcast.

Export as audio Save a session as a WAV file to play it anywhere.

Honest about the evidence The About page sums up the research in plain words: effects are modest and mixed, with the clearest results for short-term relaxation. This app is a relaxation tool, not a medical device. Before the first play it shows a short safety note about volume, driving and medical conditions.

Private by design No account, no ads, no tracking and no internet access. Your presets and settings stay on your device.

Also: light and dark theme, a reduce motion option, and English, German and Spanish.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It generates binaural, monaural, isochronic and bilateral beats live on your phone, with a beat from 0.25 to 45 Hz and a carrier from 100 to 1000 Hz. It plays sessions of 5 to 90 minutes or without a limit, and it can save a session as a WAV file.

### Does it treat or diagnose anything?

No. It is a relaxation tool and not a medical device. The About page says the research effects are modest and mixed, and a safety note about volume, driving, epilepsy and pacemakers appears before the first play.

### Where is my data stored?

Your presets and settings stay in the app's storage on your device. The app has no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet permission, so nothing leaves the phone.

### Do I need headphones?

Binaural mode needs headphones, because each ear must hear its own tone. Monaural and isochronic modes also work on speakers.

### Does it keep playing with the screen off?

Yes. A background service keeps playing with notification and lock-screen controls. The app does not pause other audio, so you can play beats quietly under music or a podcast.

### Does it need a purchase, and which Android versions work?

The app is meant to be bought once on Google Play. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. It runs on Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer.

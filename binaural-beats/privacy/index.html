Privacy Policy – Binaural Beats

App: Binaural Beats (es.schimpf.binauralbeats)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective: 9 November 2026

Short version

Binaural Beats does not collect, send or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What the app stores on your device

  • Settings: theme, the "reduce motion" switch, and whether you accepted the safety notice.
  • Your presets: the presets you save (name, tone settings, journey steps), in one file in the app's private storage.

This data never leaves your device through the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Files you choose

You can import and export presets as JSON files and export sessions as WAV audio. The app uses the Android system file picker for this. It only reads or writes the single file you pick, and it has no general access to your storage.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include your presets and settings in your device backup. The app allows cloud backup only when it is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock, and allows direct transfer to a new device. This backup is run by Android and Google under your Google account, not by me; I cannot see it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show playback controls and the time left while a session plays. Playback works without it.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep playing with the screen off or while you use other apps.
  • Wake lock: to keep the audio running while the screen is off. It is released when playback stops.

The app does not use the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or any identifiers.

Purchase

If you buy the app on Google Play, Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. Google gives me standard order details (such as order number, date, price and country) for accounting and tax. The app itself receives none of this.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

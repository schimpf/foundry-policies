Der Monarch ranks and what each rank needs

In Der Monarch, an Android strategy game set between 1720 and 1760, you start as a *Landvogt* (reeve) and try to become *Kaiser* (emperor). Each rank has fixed requirements. This page lists all of them and shows what they cost.

You get the next rank when you meet all five values at once.

"People" counts serfs, merchants, clergy and nobles together. "None" means the rank has no requirement for that item.

How promotion works

  • The check runs at the end of each year, after harvest, feeding, taxes and ships are settled.
  • You can rise one rank per year at most, even if you already meet the values for two ranks.
  • The check looks at the next rank only. You cannot skip Fürst to reach Herzog.
  • When a ruler reaches Kaiser, the game ends and that ruler wins.
  • The game also ends after the year 1760, or when every ruler is dead. Then the best standing wins. Standings sort by rank first, then by score.

What you start with

Every ruler begins in 1720 with:

  • 2,140 people (1,820 serfs, 196 merchants, 88 clergy, 36 nobles)
  • 10,000 Morgen of land
  • 1 palace wing, 0 cathedral parts, 2 markets
  • 12,400 Taler and 18,000 bushels of grain

Compared with the Kaiser line, you need about 5,900 more people, 25,000 more Morgen, 7 more palace wings, 6 cathedral parts and 6 more markets.

From the starting position, the buildings for Kaiser cost:

  • 7 palace wings: 21,000 Taler
  • 6 cathedral parts: 30,000 Taler
  • 6 markets: 6,000 Taler
  • Total: 57,000 Taler

Land is the other big cost. You buy it in steps of 100 Morgen at the year's land price, which is rolled between 1.6 and 2.8 Taler per Morgen. The 25,000 extra Morgen for Kaiser cost between 40,000 and 70,000 Taler if you buy all of it. Wars can also win land.

A worked example: Baron

Say you have 2,140 people, 1 palace wing and 12,400 Taler.

  1. Baron needs 2,500 people. You are 360 short.
  2. Baron needs 2 palace wings. You are 1 short.
  3. Build 1 palace wing for 3,000 Taler. Now only the people are missing.
  4. Feed your people well so they grow. At the start, feeding exactly the need adds about 47 people a year, feeding 125 % about 101. See the feeding guide.
  5. Once the people count passes 2,500, you become Baron at the next year end.

The Fürst step is the first one that needs a cathedral part. Note that the Build screen in the app currently says a cathedral is needed from König. The rules in the table above are what the game enforces.

Tips

  • Palace wings add nobles each year. Cathedral parts add clergy. Both help the people count.
  • Markets help merchants and sales tax income.
  • Do not spend all your Taler on buildings. Soldiers cost pay every year, and an empty treasury makes soldiers desert.
  • Your ruler ages. The slower you play a turn, the faster they age. From age 60 each year can kill them. See the sea routes chart for a fast way to raise gold.

Play it

Der Monarch is an Android game for one to four players on one device. All in-game text is in German. Open the app, pick a difficulty, and see how far you get by 1760.

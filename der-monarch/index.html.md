## About

Der Monarch is a turn-based economic strategy game set in the 18th century. It is Anno 1720 and you rule a small German realm as a humble Landvogt (reeve). By 1760 you want to wear the emperor's crown.

Please note: the game is in German only.

Every year has nine steps: - Read the year report: harvest, money, people, events and battles. - Feed your people. Too little grain and they starve or leave. Plenty draws settlers from abroad. - Plant your fields with potatoes, grain and oats, or let land lie fallow so the soil recovers. - Set customs, sales and income tax, and choose how harsh your courts are. - Buy and sell grain and land at this year's prices. - Build markets, mills, palace wings, cathedral parts, or raise new companies of soldiers. - March against a neutral neighbour or a rival ruler, or keep the peace. - Send ships on sea routes from the North Sea to China. Some come home rich with gold, grain or settlers. Some never return. - End the year and see what your orders brought.

Climb the ranks from Landvogt to Baron, Graf, Fürst, Herzog, König and finally Kaiser. Each rank needs enough people, land, palace, cathedral and markets. But time is short: your ruler ages, and from 60 on every year could be the last.

Play alone or with up to four rulers on one device, passing the phone in turn. Brandenburg, Bayern, Sachsen and Württemberg compete for the crown. A handoff screen keeps each turn private, and a standings table shows who leads.

Three difficulty levels change how good harvests are and how much grain the rats eat.

Also included: - Automatic save after every move; resume any time. - Hand-drawn illustrations and 8-bit sound effects, with a mute switch. - Light and dark design that follows your system setting. - Works on phones and tablets. - Home screen widget showing whose turn it is, the year, rank, gold and people.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no internet access, no data collection. Your game stays on your device.

## Questions and answers

### What is Der Monarch?

A turn-based economic strategy game set between 1720 and 1760. You feed your people, farm, tax, trade, build, wage war and send ships to rise from Landvogt to Kaiser.

### Is the game available in English?

No. All text in the game is German, and the game content (crops, ranks, events, sea routes) is written in German too. The guides on this site explain the terms and numbers.

### Which Android versions does it run on?

Android 8.0 and newer, on phones and tablets. It follows the light or dark setting of your system.

### Does it need internet or an account, and where is my game stored?

It needs neither. The app has no network access, no account, no analytics and asks for no permissions. Your game is saved on your device after every move and is not part of Android cloud backup, so it does not move to a new phone.

### Do I have to buy anything?

No. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no billing code.

### Can I play with other people?

Yes, with up to four rulers on one device, passing it around in turn. There is no online multiplayer, and there is one save slot.

Privacy Policy – Der Monarch

Android app es.schimpf.dermonarch
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective date: 8 November 2026

Summary

Der Monarch does not collect, send or share any personal data. The game works fully offline. Your saved game stays on your device.

What the app stores

  • One saved game (rulers' names you type in, the realm, and the game state such as gold, grain, land and buildings).
  • Whether sound is switched on or off.

Both are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read them.

What the app does not do

  • No internet access. The app does not request the internet permission and cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts or file access.

Backup and device transfer

The saved game is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. It is not copied to Google or any other service.

Home screen widget

If you add the optional widget, it shows whose turn it is, the year, rank, gold and population on your home screen. Anyone who can see your home screen can see this. The data still stays on your device.

Deleting your data

Finishing a game and starting a new one clears the saved game. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all its data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

## About

Dyslexia Reader is a book reader made for people with dyslexia. Import your own books, then set up the text so it is comfortable for your eyes.

## Features

### Reading tools

- Line focus ruler: dims the lines around the one you are reading, so you keep your place.
- Dyslexia-friendly fonts: Lexend, OpenDyslexic and Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- Letter, word and line spacing sliders, plus text size.
- Reading presets for background colour, text colour, font and spacing.
- Read aloud with the voice installed on your device. The current sentence is highlighted and you can change the speed. Reading can continue with the screen off.
- Light and dark mode follow your system setting.

### Your books

- Import TXT, EPUB and PDF files from your device.
- Share plain text from another app to add it to your library.
- PDF files are turned into text, so your font and spacing apply. Images and page layout are not kept. Scanned PDFs without text are not supported.
- A short sample book is included, so you can try every feature before adding your own books.
- Your library shows progress and time left for each book. A home-screen widget shows your reading progress and takes you back to your place.

### Made to be accessible

- Large touch targets and labels for screen readers.
- Works on phones and tablets.

### Privacy

- No account is needed.
- Your books and settings stay on your device. The app has no internet permission, no ads and no analytics.
- You can delete a single book or all app data at any time in Settings.
- An option in Settings blocks screenshots.

### Free and paid features

Free: TXT and EPUB import, shared text, the Lexend font, the basic line focus ruler, spacing sliders and the widget. Unlock with one purchase through Google Play: all fonts, read aloud with sentence highlight, reading presets, a customisable ruler (height, dimming and tint) and PDF import. The purchase is made once, needs no account, and can be restored with the same Google account.

Fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License. Licence details are in Settings.

## Questions and answers

### What does Dyslexia Reader do?

It reads TXT, EPUB and PDF files and plain text shared from other apps. You choose a dyslexia-friendly font (Lexend, OpenDyslexic or Atkinson Hyperlegible), set letter, word and line spacing, use a line focus ruler, and listen with read aloud that highlights the current sentence.

### What does it not do?

It does not do OCR, so scanned PDFs without text are not supported. PDFs are turned into plain text, so images and page layout are not kept. It does not highlight single words while reading aloud.

### Where are my books and settings stored?

Only on your device. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics, and backup is switched off. You can delete one book or all app data in Settings.

### What is free and what needs the purchase?

TXT and EPUB import, shared text, the Lexend font, the basic ruler, spacing sliders and the widget are free. The other fonts, read aloud, reading presets, the adjustable ruler and PDF import need one purchase through Google Play. It needs no account and can be restored with the same Google account.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 10 or newer. It runs on phones and tablets.

### Can I try it before buying?

Yes. A short sample book is included, and you can add it from the empty Library. It lets you try the features before you add your own books.

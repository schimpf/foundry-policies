Privacy Policy – Dyslexia Reader

Effective: 2026-11-14

App: Dyslexia Reader (package es.schimpf.dyslexiareaderandroid)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es

Short version

The developer does not collect, receive or share any data from this app. Your books and settings stay on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Books you add (the extracted text, title, author and a cover colour). The original file is not kept.
  • Your reading position in each book.
  • Your settings: font, spacing, reading presets, ruler options, reading speed, library sort and filter.
  • Whether the paid upgrade is unlocked on this device.

This data lives in the app's private storage. It is not sent anywhere. Backup and device transfer are turned off for the app, so Android does not copy this data to the cloud or to a new phone.

Network use

The app does not have internet permission. It cannot send data over the network. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

Google Play purchase

Some features need a one-time purchase. The purchase is handled by the Google Play Store app on your device. Google processes payment and purchase data under its own privacy policy. The developer does not see your payment details. This app only asks Google Play whether the purchase exists, and keeps the result on your device.

Files and shared text

You choose files with the Android file picker, or share plain text to the app. The app reads only what you pick or share. It requests no storage permission. Links, scripts and images in files are removed.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The app prefers voices that work without a network. The engine is provided by your device or by another app, and its own privacy policy applies. The app shows a notification while it reads aloud so reading can continue with the screen off.

Widget

The optional home-screen widget shows your reading progress. It can show the book title or only the percentage. You can hide the title in Settings.

Screenshots

Settings has an option to block screenshots and hide the app in the recent apps list.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single book from the Library.
  • Use "Delete all data" in Settings to remove all books, positions, presets and settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Your purchase stays with your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Privacy Policy

App: Grief Letter Journal (es.schimpf.griefletterjournal)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

The short version

The developer does not collect, receive or see any of your data. Everything you write stays on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The people you add: name, relation, and optional birthday and date of death.
  • Your letters, and whether each one is kept or sealed.
  • An optional photo per person. You pick it with the system photo picker; the app gets only the photo you choose, saves only the part inside the circle, and removes location and camera details.
  • Settings: app lock on or off, lock timeout, reminder time, and whether the purchase is unlocked.

People, letters and photos are kept in an encrypted database (SQLCipher). Its key is protected by the Android Keystore. This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Network and third parties

  • The app has no INTERNET permission and no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking.
  • The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. The Google Play Store app checks and processes the purchase, under Google's own privacy policy. The app only receives the result (unlocked or not) and keeps it on your phone. The developer receives no payment details from you.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for optional birthday and anniversary reminders, which are off by default. The text is neutral and contains no names or dates, and it is hidden on the lock screen.
  • Biometrics: for the optional app lock. The fingerprint or face check is done by Android; the app never sees your biometric data.
  • Google Play billing: to offer the one-time unlock.

The app does not request storage, camera, location, contacts or microphone permissions.

Files you create

  • Backups: you can create a backup file protected with a passphrase you choose, and save it wherever you like using the system file picker. Without the passphrase the file cannot be opened, not even by the developer. Keep the passphrase safe: it cannot be recovered.
  • PDF export: a PDF of your letters is saved where you choose. It is not encrypted. Anyone with the file can read it.

Once you save a file outside the app, it is under your control and no longer protected by the app.

Widget and screenshots

  • The home screen widget never shows letter text. With app lock on, it shows only a plain "Write" button. With app lock off, it can show a person's name and photo.
  • While app lock is on, screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked.

Deleting your data

  • Release a letter to delete it permanently.
  • Delete a person to permanently remove that person, their letters and their photo.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored by it. Backup and PDF files you saved yourself must be deleted by you.

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from or delete at the developer's side.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

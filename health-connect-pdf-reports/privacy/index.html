Privacy Policy

App: Health Reports (Health Connect PDF Reports), package es.schimpf.healthconnectpdfreports
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-18

Short version: the app works on your phone only. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer receives none of your data.

What the app reads

With your permission in Health Connect, the app reads heart rate, resting heart rate, blood pressure and sleep records. It reads only the date range you choose for a report, plus the last days shown on the home screen and the widget. Reading data older than 30 days needs the optional history permission. Reading in the background for the home screen widget needs the optional background permission. You can refuse both and still use the app.

What the app writes

If you add a blood pressure reading by hand, the app saves it to Health Connect, with a pulse value if you enter one and allow it. Other apps that you allowed to read blood pressure in Health Connect can see it. You can edit or delete it in Health Connect. The app writes nothing else.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your preferred data source for each metric.
  • Your name, only if you tick "Remember on this device". It is encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore. You can clear it in Settings.
  • Whether the free export was used, and whether the unlock is owned (by purchase or by an unlock code).
  • The last values the widget shows (latest blood pressure and 7-day average resting heart rate).
  • A temporary PDF file in the app's cache. It is deleted when you leave the preview and when the app starts.

The app keeps no other copy of your health data. Health Connect remains the only store. Android backup and device transfer are switched off for the app.

Sharing

The app shares a report only when you tap Share or Print. The PDF then goes to the app or printer that you pick in the Android share sheet or print dialog. The developer does not receive it. What the receiving app or service does with the PDF is governed by its own policy. The PDF contains your health values and, if you typed one, your name.

Network use

The app does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot send data over the network itself. The only outside contact is the Google Play Billing library, which talks to the Google Play app on your phone for the unlock purchase.

Purchases

Unlimited exports are a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles payment and its own records under its own privacy policy. The app only learns whether you own the unlock. The developer does not see your payment details.

Analytics, ads and third parties

None. The app has no ad, analytics, tracking or crash reporting code. It does not sell or share data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps what is listed above until you remove it. Clear your saved name in Settings, remove the app's access in Health Connect settings, or uninstall the app to delete everything it stored. Readings you saved to Health Connect stay there until you delete them in Health Connect. Reports you already shared stay with whoever received them.

Not medical advice

The app shows recorded values only. It gives no diagnosis and no medical advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Privacy Policy

Health Reports (package es.schimpf.healthconnectpdfreports)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf · Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 13 November 2026

Summary

Health Reports runs entirely on your phone. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What the app reads

With your permission in Health Connect, the app reads heart rate, resting heart rate, blood pressure and sleep records, only for the date range you choose for a report. It reads records older than 30 days only if you allow history access. For the home screen widget, it can also read data in the background if you allow that permission.

What the app writes

If you enter a blood pressure reading by hand, the app saves it, and the pulse if you entered one, to Health Connect. It writes nothing else. Other apps that you have given access in Health Connect can see these entries. You can edit or delete them in Health Connect.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your preferred data source for each metric.
  • Your name, only if you choose to remember it. It is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. You can remove it in Settings.
  • Whether the free export has been used and whether you unlocked unlimited exports.
  • The last blood pressure reading and the 7-day average resting heart rate shown by the widget.

The app does not keep a copy of your health data. A PDF report is created as a temporary file in the app's private cache. It is deleted when you leave the preview and when the app starts.

Sharing

The app only sends a report somewhere when you tap Share or Print and pick an app or printer in the Android share sheet or print dialog. From then on, the receiving app or service handles the file under its own privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

Network use and third parties

  • The app declares no internet permission and makes no network connections.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • Data is not backed up to the cloud and is excluded from device transfer.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. The app uses the Google Play Billing library only to learn whether you own the unlock. The developer does not receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play.

Your control

  • Remove the app's access at any time in Health Connect settings.
  • Delete the saved name in the app's Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Health Connect data belongs to Health Connect and the apps that wrote it. Manage it there.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

The app shows your recorded data only. It gives no diagnosis and no medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Horse Care Log – Privacy Policy

App: Horse Care Log (es.schimpf.horsecarelog)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective date: 8 November 2026

Summary

Horse Care Log works fully offline. It has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your device. The developer does not collect, receive or see any of your data.

What the app stores on your device

  • Horse profiles: name, photo, breed, birth date, sex, colour, microchip and passport numbers, notes.
  • Care schedules and log entries: farrier, worming, vaccines (product, batch number, vet name), vet notes.
  • Daily feed rations and costs.
  • Photos and PDF files you choose to attach.
  • Settings: weight unit, reminder on/off, whether Pro is unlocked, date of your last backup.

This data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's device encryption protects it. Photos you add are re-encoded and resized, which removes embedded metadata such as GPS location.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to remind you 3 days before and on the day care is due. On the lock screen the reminder shows only a count, not your horse's name. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in Android settings.
  • Google Play billing – to sell the optional one-time Pro unlock.

The app does not use the camera, location, contacts or shared storage permissions. Photos and files are picked through the Android system file picker, which only gives the app the single file you choose.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app only asks the Google Play app on your device whether Pro was bought. The developer receives no payment card details from the app. Google's handling of the purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Backups, PDF export and sharing

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app. Data only leaves the app when you do so on purpose:

  • Backup (Pro) – you choose where to save a single backup file through the system file picker. You can encrypt it with a password (AES-256-GCM). The password is never stored. If you save the file to a cloud folder, that provider's privacy terms apply.
  • PDF export (Pro) – you choose to save or share a PDF with your horse's vaccine and vet history. It goes only to the app or place you pick.

Data sharing

No data is sold or shared with the developer or any third party.

Deleting your data

You can delete single entries, files or a whole horse in the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of its data from the device. Backup and PDF files you saved elsewhere are not touched; delete them yourself if you want them gone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

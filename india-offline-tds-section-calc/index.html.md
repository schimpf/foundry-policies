## About

Work out non-salary TDS in seconds. Pick the payment type, enter the amount and see the TDS, the rate, the net payable and the section, under both numbering systems.

TDS Section Calc covers FY 2025-26 (Income-tax Act 1961) and FY 2026-27 (Income-tax Act 2025). Every result shows the old and new section numbers side by side, so you can move to the new Act without a lookup table.

The app has no internet access. Rates, thresholds and section numbers are bundled with the app and change only with app updates.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app calculate?

It calculates non-salary TDS by payment type for FY 2025-26 (Income-tax Act 1961) and FY 2026-27 (Income-tax Act 2025). Each result shows the TDS, the rate, the net payable and the old and new section numbers. Salary TDS (s.192), TCS, non-resident payments and return filing are not covered.

### Does it need an internet connection?

No. The app has no internet permission. Rates, thresholds and section numbers are bundled with the app and change only with app updates.

### Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. There is no account and no tracking. Saved payees are encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore, and the app is excluded from cloud backup. Data leaves the phone only when you export a sheet or create a backup file.

### What is free and what needs a purchase?

The calculator, rate chart, due-date calendar and home screen widget are free, without ads. Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. It adds saved payees, certificates, history, PDF and Excel export, reminders, the interest and late-fee calculator, backup and app lock.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 or newer. The layout adapts to phones and tablets, and follows the system light or dark theme.

### Are the results tax advice?

No. The results are estimates. Check the Act, rules and notifications before you deduct, deposit or file.

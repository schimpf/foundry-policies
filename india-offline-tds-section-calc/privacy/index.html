Privacy Policy

TDS Section Calc (package es.schimpf.indiaofflinetdssectioncalc)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective: 13 November 2026

Summary

The app does not collect your data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Nothing you enter is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Network access

The app does not have the INTERNET permission. It cannot connect to any server. Tax rates, thresholds, section numbers and due dates are bundled inside the app and change only when you install an app update.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Free features (calculator, rate chart, due dates, widget): nothing you enter is saved. Only app settings are kept, such as the financial year you picked.
  • Pro features: payee names, PAN, payee type, default section, notes, lower-deduction certificates and saved calculations. They are stored in a database encrypted with SQLCipher. The key is protected by the Android Keystore. They stay on your phone.
  • Settings: reminder options, app lock on or off, and whether Pro is unlocked.

The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If the Keystore key is lost, for example after a device restore, the saved payees cannot be read and you can reset them or restore from your own backup file.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app only asks Google Play whether you own Pro. It never sees your payment details. Google processes the purchase under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): optional Pro due-date reminders. They show dates only, never names or PAN.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): to buy and restore Pro through Google Play.
  • Biometric or screen lock: optional Pro app lock, handled by Android. The app never receives your fingerprint, face or PIN.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Files you create

  • Export (Pro): a PDF or Excel sheet is created in the app's cache and handed to the Android share sheet. Where it goes is your choice. PAN is masked by default. The app deletes its copy later.
  • Backup (Pro): an encrypted file (AES-256-GCM, key derived from your password). It is saved where you choose with the system file picker. The app keeps no copy. The password cannot be recovered.

Sharing

The developer receives no data and shares none. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Deleting your data

Delete a payee or a saved calculation inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data it stored. Files you exported or backed up are outside the app. Delete them yourself.

Children

The app is a tax tool for businesses and professionals and is not directed at children.

Not tax advice

Results are estimates. Check the Act, rules and notifications before you deduct, deposit or file.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

TDS Due Dates and Late Interest Calculation

This page lists the TDS due dates for non-salary payments in FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27. It then shows how late interest and the late filing fee are worked out, with an example.

TDS deposit due dates

Deposit TDS to the government by the 7th of the month after the month of deduction. TDS deducted in March has a later date: 30 April.

Interest for late TDS

Two kinds of interest apply under section 201(1A) of the Income-tax Act, 1961:

  • Late deduction: 1% per month or part of a month, from the date the tax was deductible to the date it was deducted.
  • Late deposit: 1.5% per month or part of a month, from the date of deduction to the date of deposit. It applies only when the deposit is after the due date.

Both are charged on the TDS amount, not on the payment.

How the app counts months

The app counts each calendar month touched by the period. A period from 15 November to 12 December touches two months, November and December. So it counts 2 months. The app counts a period only when the end date is later than the start date.

Late filing fee

Section 234E charges ₹200 for each day a TDS return is late. The fee cannot be more than the TDS amount for that return. The days run from the return due date to the date you file.

Worked example

You deduct ₹10,000 of TDS on 15 November 2025. The deposit is due on 7 December 2025. You deposit on 12 December 2025.

  • Months touched, 15 Nov to 12 Dec: November and December = 2
  • Interest: 10,000 × 1.5% × 2 = ₹300

Now the Q2 return was due on 31 October 2025 and you file on 10 November 2025.

  • Days late: 10
  • Fee: 10 × ₹200 = ₹2,000
  • Cap: the TDS amount, ₹10,000. The fee stays at ₹2,000.

Now suppose you also deducted late. The tax was deductible on 10 October but you deducted on 5 November.

  • Months touched: October and November = 2
  • Interest: 10,000 × 1% × 2 = ₹200

Total for all three: 300 + 2,000 + 200 = ₹2,500.

If the fee were above the TDS amount, the TDS amount would be used instead. For TDS of ₹1,000 and 10 days late, the fee would be ₹1,000, not ₹2,000.

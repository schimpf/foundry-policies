Privacy Policy

Job Loss 12-Week Plan (package es.schimpf.jobloss12weekplan)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective: 2026-11-13

Short version

The app does not collect your data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. I never receive it.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your plan: layoff date, plan start date, tasks and notes, deadlines.
  • Your money details: savings, severance, weekly unemployment benefit, budget categories.
  • Your weekly reviews (rest, mood, note) and application counts.
  • Settings: app lock on or off, reminder times, and whether Pro is unlocked.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not encrypted by the app itself. It relies on Android's device protection. The optional app lock controls who can open the app. It does not encrypt the data.

Network use

The app does not request the internet permission and cannot connect to any server of mine or of anyone else. The only outside contact is the Pro purchase, which is handled by Google Play Billing through the Play Store app on your device. I do not see your payment details or your Google account. Google processes purchases under its own Privacy Policy.

Links to official US sources (for example dol.gov and healthcare.gov) open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Notifications: local reminders for deadlines and the weekly review. They are created on your device and never show amounts.
  • Biometric / screen lock: the optional app lock uses the Android system prompt. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data.
  • Google Play Billing: to buy and restore Pro.

Backups and files you create

  • Android cloud backup and device transfer are turned off for this app.
  • You can export a backup file (JSON) and a PDF (Pro). They are saved only where you choose, using the system file picker. The backup file is plain text and not encrypted. It contains your savings, budget and notes, so store it somewhere private.
  • You can import a backup file. It replaces the data in the app after you confirm.

Widget

The home screen widget shows your current week, open tasks, next deadline and runway. While the app lock is on, it hides the runway.

Sharing

I do not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone, because I do not have it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings, and all data is removed. Files you exported yourself stay where you saved them, and you can delete them there.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Not advice

The app gives general US information only. It is not legal or financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.

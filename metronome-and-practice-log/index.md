## About

Press play and practise. Metronome & Practice Log keeps time and writes your practice diary for you.

## Features

### A metronome you can rely on

- Tempo from 30 to 300 BPM with large minus and plus buttons, a slider and tap tempo
- Time signatures 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4 and 6/8, with accents you set for each beat
- Three sounds: click, wood and beep
- Screen flash and vibration on the beat, with one simple switch for both
- Steady timing in the background and with the screen off, with a notification to pause

### Practice logging without extra taps

- A session starts when you press play and ends when you pause
- Sessions shorter than 30 seconds are ignored
- Assign a piece, or log to "General"
- Each session saves its time signature, your last tempo and the time-weighted average tempo
- Edit or delete any session, with undo
- Add, rename and delete your pieces

### See your progress

- Daily streak: every day with at least one minute of practice counts
- Bar chart of your last 7 days

### Pro (ONE-TIME PURCHASE)

These features need the Pro purchase:

The metronome, automatic logging, streaks and the 7-day chart work without it.

- Export your log as CSV or JSON to a file you choose
- Full graphs: 12 weeks of practice time and tempo progress for each piece
- Accent colour themes
- Home-screen widget with your streak, today's time and the last 7 days

### Your data stays on your device

- The app has no internet permission
- No account, no analytics, no crash reporting
- No cloud backup of your practice data. It stays on this device unless you export it.

### Made to be easy to use

Contact: apps@schimpf.es

- Follows your system light or dark mode
- Works with TalkBack: controls are labelled, the tempo is one adjustable control, and charts have a spoken summary
- Large touch targets for quick use on a music stand
- Phone and tablet layouts. On tablets, the metronome and your log sit side by side.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It is a metronome from 30 to 300 BPM with tap tempo, time signatures 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4 and 6/8, and three sounds. It also logs your practice automatically and counts a daily streak.

### How does it log my practice?

A session starts when you press play and ends when you pause. Sessions under 30 seconds are ignored, and each one saves its piece, time signature, last tempo and time-weighted average tempo. You can edit or delete any session.

### Where is my data stored?

On your device only. The app has no internet permission, no account, no analytics and no cloud backup, and your data leaves the device only if you export it.

### What needs the Pro purchase?

Export as CSV or JSON, the full graphs (12 weeks and tempo progress per piece), accent colour themes and the home-screen widget. The metronome, automatic logging, streaks and the 7-day chart are free.

### Does it keep time with the screen off?

Yes. It keeps steady time in the background and with the screen off, and a notification lets you pause it.

### Which Android versions does it support?

Android 8.0 or newer, on phones and tablets.

Privacy Policy

Metronome & Practice Log (es.schimpf.metronomeandpracticelog)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective: 2026-11-13

Summary

The app does not collect, send or share your data. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

  • Your practice sessions: start time, duration, time signature, last and average tempo, and the piece name you chose.
  • The names of your pieces.
  • Your settings: sound, flash and vibration, accent theme, last tempo and time signature.
  • A signed marker that says whether Pro is unlocked on this device.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. The developer cannot see it.

No network access

The app does not have the Android internet permission. It cannot send data over the network by itself.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There is no account or sign-in. The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking.

Backups

Android backup is turned off for this app. Your practice data is not copied to cloud backup or to a new device during device transfer. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, the data is gone unless you exported it.

Purchases

Pro features are unlocked with a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no payment details from you. The app asks the Google Play app on your device whether Pro is owned, and keeps a signed yes/no marker on the device. It does not store purchase tokens. A reviewer unlock code can also unlock Pro. It is checked on the device only and is never sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): to check and complete the Pro purchase.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep the metronome steady with the screen off or the app in the background.
  • Notifications: to show the ongoing metronome notification with a pause control. It shows only the tempo, not your piece name.
  • Vibration: to vibrate on the beat if you turn that on.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Export (Pro)

You can export your log as CSV or JSON. You pick where the file is saved, using Android's file picker. After that, the file is outside the app's protection and is handled by whatever app or service holds that location. The app does not upload it.

Home-screen widget (Pro)

The widget shows your streak, today's practice time and the last 7 days. It reads the data on your device only.

Deleting your data

You can delete single sessions and pieces inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. Files you exported are not removed by uninstalling; delete those yourself. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to delete on a server.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

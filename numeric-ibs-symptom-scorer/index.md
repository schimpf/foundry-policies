## About

Numeric IBS Symptom Scorer is a private diary for people with irritable bowel syndrome, or suspected IBS, who want clear numbers to track their symptoms for themselves or for their doctor.

Exact numbers, no sliders Rate abdominal pain, bloating, gas, cramping and nausea from 0 to 10 by tapping a number or typing it. Add up to five symptoms of your own. Log as many entries per day as you like, each with an optional note. Leave a symptom unrated if you did not notice it.

A proper bowel log Record each bowel movement with the Bristol stool type (drawn icons plus text), urgency from 0 to 10, complete or incomplete emptying, an optional blood or mucus flag and a note.

Food log with tags Write down what you ate and add your own reusable tags, such as dairy, onion or coffee.

Today view and charts See the whole day in time order and edit or delete any entry. Charts show the daily average and maximum per symptom, bowel movements per day and your Bristol type distribution over 7, 30 or 90 days or all time, with no history limit. Every chart also has a written summary.

Widget and reminder The home screen widget shows today's latest scores and bowel count, with quick-add buttons. An optional daily reminder helps you keep the habit.

Private by design No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. The app has no internet permission. Your diary is stored in an encrypted database on your phone. An optional app lock uses your fingerprint, face, screen lock or an app PIN, and while it is on, the app is hidden in the recent apps view.

Pro, a one-time purchase • Trigger report: your average score a few hours after each food tag, compared with your usual level, with the number of meals behind it. • Export a PDF report for your doctor or CSV files for any date range. • Encrypted, password-protected backup and restore, saved wherever you choose. Logging, charts, the widget, the reminder and the app lock stay free.

Light and dark theme, phone and tablet layouts, and full screen reader support.

This app is not medical advice and does not diagnose anything. Food links it shows are possible links only. Talk to your doctor about your symptoms.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It is a diary where you rate abdominal pain, bloating, gas, cramping and nausea from 0 to 10 by tapping or typing a number, plus up to five symptoms of your own. You also log bowel movements (Bristol type 1 to 7, urgency 0 to 10) and food with tags, and see charts over 7, 30 or 90 days or all time.

### Does it diagnose IBS or tell me which foods to avoid?

No. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose anything. The trigger report shows your average score after each food tag compared with your usual level, and labels results as possible links only.

### Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in an encrypted database. There is no account, no cloud, no ads and no analytics, and the app has no internet permission. Android auto-backup is turned off, so your diary only leaves the phone if you export it or save an encrypted backup.

### What needs Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play. It adds the trigger report, PDF and CSV export and password-protected backup and restore. Logging, the timeline, all charts, the widget, the reminder and the app lock are free.

### Which Android versions does it support?

Android 11 or newer. It has phone and tablet layouts, a light and dark theme and screen reader support.

### Is the app available in my language?

Not yet. The app itself is English only, and the Play Store page is also available in German and Spanish.

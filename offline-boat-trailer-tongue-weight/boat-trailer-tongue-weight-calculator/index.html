Boat Trailer Tongue Weight Calculator and Formula

Estimate boat trailer tongue weight from item weights and positions. Formula, worked example, the 7 to 10 percent target and a small calculator.

Tongue weight is the downward force the trailer coupler puts on the hitch ball. For a boat trailer, the usual target is 7 to 10 percent of the total trailer weight. This page shows how to estimate it from the weight and position of each item on the trailer, with a worked example and a calculator you can use right here.

What tongue weight is

The trailer rests on its axle (or axles) and on the hitch. Whatever the axle does not carry, the hitch carries. That share is the tongue weight.

  • Too little (under about 5 percent) and the trailer can sway.
  • Too much (over about 15 percent) and the rear of the tow vehicle sags and the front lifts, which hurts steering and braking.
  • 7 to 10 percent is the usual target for boat trailers.

The 7 to 10 percent range is a common rule of thumb and no legal limit. Your trailer maker and hitch maker may give a different range, so check the manuals. The hard limit is the maximum tongue weight printed on your hitch and receiver labels.

The formula

The estimate is a moment balance about the trailer axle. Measure every position in inches from the axle. A position toward the coupler is positive. A position toward the stern is negative.

  1. Moment of each item = weight x position from the axle.
  2. Total moment = sum of all item moments, plus the moment of the empty trailer.
  3. Tongue weight = total moment / coupler-to-axle distance.
  4. Tongue weight % = tongue weight / total trailer weight x 100.

Total trailer weight is the empty trailer plus every item on it. For a tandem trailer, measure to the middle point between the two axles.

The empty trailer

The bare frame also has weight and a balance point. If you do not know it, a reasonable assumption is that the bare frame balances at 20 percent of the coupler-to-axle distance, ahead of the axle. The calculator below uses that value unless you change it.

Worked example

Take a tandem trailer with a coupler-to-axle distance of 168 inches (14 feet).

  • Tongue weight = 51,560 / 168 = 306.9 lb
  • Tongue weight % = 306.9 / 3,794 = 8.1 percent

That is inside the 7 to 10 percent target.

Check the fuel line: 40 gallons of gasoline at 6.1 lb per gallon is 244 lb. See boat fuel and water weight per gallon for the weights of other liquids.

Axle load in the same example

The axles carry everything except the tongue weight: 3,794 - 306.9 = 3,487.1 lb. On a tandem trailer that is about 1,743.5 lb per axle. Compare it with the axle rating on the trailer VIN plate. The app shows Warn when a load reaches 90 percent of a rating.

Reading the result

A simple way to read the percent:

Tongue weight %Meaning
Under 5Too light, sway risk
5 to under 7Light, adjust if you can
7 to 10Target
Over 10 to 15Heavy, adjust if you can
Over 15Too heavy

Also check the tongue weight against the maximum on your hitch and receiver, and the full trailer weight against the tow rating of your vehicle and the trailer GVWR.

Accuracy

This is an estimate. It is only as good as the weights and distances you enter.

  • Use real weights from spec sheets or a scale.
  • Measure each position to the center of mass of the item. For a hull, that is hard to know, so treat the hull number as the weakest input.
  • Liquids move. Fuel and water change both weight and balance as you use them.
  • Weigh the loaded rig on a certified scale when in doubt, or when the result is near a limit.

If the result is outside 7 to 10 percent, see how to increase boat trailer tongue weight for ways to fix it.

Do it in the app

The Boat Trailer Tongue Weight app for Android does this for your whole rig. You enter the ratings from your door sticker, hitch label and trailer VIN plate, then add the hull, engine, fuel, water and gear with their positions. It shows the dry and loaded trailer side by side, checks each rating as Pass, Warn or Fail, and suggests changes to reach 7 to 10 percent. It works offline, so you can use it at the ramp.

Privacy Policy

Boat Trailer Tongue Weight (es.schimpf.offlineboattrailertongueweight)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective: 2026-11-13

Summary

The app does not collect, send or share any data. It has no internet access, no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your rigs: rig names, vehicle, trailer and load item weights, positions and ratings that you type in.
  • Your settings: unit system (imperial or metric), whether you accepted the safety notice, and which rig you used last.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded anywhere. The app turns off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for this data, so it is not copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not have the internet permission. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Network use

None. The app cannot connect to the internet. The developer receives nothing from the app.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking software. No data is shared with third parties. Google Play handles the purchase and installation of the app under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

PDF export and sharing

When you choose Export PDF, the app creates a PDF summary of the selected rig on your device. The file is kept in a temporary folder in the app's private cache and old files are deleted automatically. The PDF leaves your device only if you tap Share or Save. Then you choose the target (for example an email app or a folder), and that app or service handles the file under its own policy.

Home screen widget

The optional widget shows the tongue weight and pass or fail result of your last-used rig. It reads this from the app's local data on your device and sends nothing out.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You control your data on your device and can delete a rig in the app or delete everything by uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date changed before the update is released.

## About

Fire extinguisher inspection for service techs and facility managers who work to NFPA 10. Extinguisher Tagging keeps your clients, sites and extinguishers on your phone, walks you through the monthly and annual checklists, and builds a signed PDF report for each site. The app needs no account and has no internet permission, so it works in basements and plant rooms without signal.

Scan and inspect Scan the QR code or barcode on an existing tag with the camera. A known tag opens that extinguisher. An unknown tag offers to add a new extinguisher with the code already filled in. If a label is damaged, type the code by hand. Start an inspection round at a site to scan, inspect and move to the next unit without going back to the list. The monthly checklist follows NFPA 10 section 7.2 and the annual checklist follows section 7.3. Mark each item pass or fail. A failed item needs a reason, and you can add a corrective action and mark the unit as replaced. Attach up to 3 photos to each inspection.

Due dates For every extinguisher the app records the last monthly, annual, 6-year maintenance and hydrostatic test dates, and works out the next dates from the extinguisher type. Each unit, site and client shows OK, due soon or overdue. The app sends one reminder per site once a day. The home screen widget shows the overdue count, the due soon count and the next site to visit.

Reports and QR stickers Every fire extinguisher inspection ends up in the site report: each extinguisher with its result, failure details and photo thumbnails, plus your logo, company name, technician name and license number. Sign on the screen, then send the PDF through the share sheet. For untagged units, the app creates sheets of new QR codes for Avery 22806, 5160 and 94102 labels. A code is never used twice.

Your data Clients, photos and signatures stay on your device in an encrypted database. Create a backup .zip with an optional AES-256 password and restore it later, or export and import your extinguisher list as a CSV file.

Free version and Pro The free version has 1 client, 25 extinguishers and a FREE VERSION watermark on report PDFs. Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play with no subscription. It removes the limits on clients and extinguishers and removes the watermark. If you reach a limit, your existing data is not deleted or hidden.

The app also works on tablets, follows the light or dark system theme, and uses large touch targets for work with gloves. Use it as an extinguisher inspection checklist and log. It records inspections and does not replace a qualified technician or the rules of your authority having jurisdiction.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It runs the NFPA 10 monthly and annual checklists for each extinguisher, tracks the next monthly, annual, 6-year and hydrostatic test dates, and builds a signed PDF report for each site. You scan the QR code or barcode on a tag with the camera to open a unit.

### Does it work without a signal or an account?

Yes. There is no account or login, and the app has no internet permission, so it works in basements and plant rooms.

### Where is my data stored?

Clients, sites, photos and signatures stay on your phone in an encrypted database. You can save a backup .zip with an optional AES-256 password, or export and import the extinguisher list as a CSV file.

### What is free and what needs Pro?

The free version has 1 client, 25 extinguishers and a FREE VERSION watermark on report PDFs. Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, with no subscription, and removes the limits and the watermark.

### What does the app not do?

It has no cloud sync and no checklists for 6-year maintenance and hydrostatic tests, only their dates. It follows the US standard NFPA 10, the checklists are in English, and it does not replace a qualified technician or the rules of your authority having jurisdiction.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer, on phones and tablets.

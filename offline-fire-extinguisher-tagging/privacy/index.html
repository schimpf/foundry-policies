Privacy Policy

Extinguisher Tagging (Android package es.schimpf.offlinefireextinguishertagging)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective: 2026-11-27

Summary

The app does not send your data anywhere. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no internet permission. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

You can enter your company name, technician name, license number, an optional logo, client and site names, addresses and notes, extinguisher details, inspection results, photos, and a signature. The app stores this data only in its private storage on your device. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher, and photos, signatures, the logo and stored reports are encrypted with AES-GCM. The keys are held in the Android Keystore. The developer cannot see or access this data.

Permissions

  • Camera: reads QR codes and barcodes on tags, and takes inspection photos. Codes are read on the device by a bundled ML Kit model. Photos are re-encoded without location data and stored encrypted in the app. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Notifications: shows reminders for due and overdue extinguishers. The check runs on the device once a day. You can decline this permission and keep using the app.
  • Google Play Billing: lets you buy the Pro upgrade through the Google Play Store app.

The app does not request location, contacts, storage or internet permission.

Network use

The app itself makes no network connections. The Android internet permission is removed from the app. The purchase and the optional Play rating prompt are handled by the Google Play Store app on your device, which has its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app keeps a local copy of whether Pro is active, so it works without a connection.

The Settings screen has a "More apps" entry. It opens apps.schimpf.es in your browser only when you tap it. The link carries a campaign tag that names this app, and no personal data.

Sharing

The app shares nothing with the developer or third parties. When you create a report PDF, backup file, CSV file or sticker sheet, the Android share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes. A temporary copy of the file is kept in the app's cache for sharing and is removed on the next app start or by the daily check. A backup without a password can be read by anyone who gets the file. The app warns you before it creates one.

Analytics and ads

None. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a client, site or extinguisher in the app, which also removes its inspections, photos and reports. Uninstalling the app removes all remaining app data. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app. A backup file you create yourself is under your control, and you must delete it yourself.

Children

The app is a work tool for fire-safety inspections and is not directed at children.

Changes

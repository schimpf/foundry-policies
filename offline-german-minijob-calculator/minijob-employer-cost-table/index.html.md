# Minijob Employer Cost and Contribution Rates Table

> What a German Minijob costs the employer, with pension, health, tax, U1, U2 and more. Rates table and worked examples for commercial and household.

- Web: https://apps.schimpf.es/offline-german-minijob-calculator/minijob-employer-cost-table/
- App: Minijob Calculator Germany, https://apps.schimpf.es/offline-german-minijob-calculator/index.md
Besides the wage, an employer pays flat-rate contributions on every Minijob. This page lists each rate, explains how the total is built and gives a worked example with 603 EUR, the 2026 monthly limit.

## Rates table

All rates are a percentage of the gross pay.

| Line | Commercial employer | Private household (Haushaltsscheck) |
|---|---|---|
| Pension insurance | 15.00% | 5.00% |
| Health insurance | 13.00% | 5.00% |
| Flat-rate tax | 2.00% | 2.00% |
| U1 (sick pay levy) | 0.80% | 0.80% |
| U2 (maternity levy) | 0.22% | 0.22% |
| Insolvency levy | 0.15% | none |
| Accident insurance | Set by the trade association (Berufsgenossenschaft), enter your own rate | 1.60% |

- The app uses the same rates for 2026 and 2027.
- The 2027 values are provisional until officially confirmed.
- The U1 and U2 rates are set by the Minijob-Zentrale and can differ by fund and by chosen reimbursement level. The values here are the standard rates the app uses.
- Source: Minijob-Zentrale, rates and contributions for employers (minijob-zentrale.de). Always check the current rates before you pay.

## How the total is built

1. Take the gross pay for the month.
2. For each line, multiply the pay by the rate and round to the cent (half up).
3. Add all rounded lines to get the contributions.
4. Total cost = pay + contributions.

Each line is rounded on its own, and the total is the sum of the rounded lines.

## Worked example: commercial employer

Pay is 603.00 EUR. For accident insurance we assume a rate of 1.50% as an example. Your own rate comes from your Berufsgenossenschaft.

| Line | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Pension | 603.00 x 15.00% | 90.45 EUR |
| Health | 603.00 x 13.00% | 78.39 EUR |
| Flat-rate tax | 603.00 x 2.00% | 12.06 EUR |
| U1 | 603.00 x 0.80% = 4.824 | 4.82 EUR |
| U2 | 603.00 x 0.22% = 1.3266 | 1.33 EUR |
| Insolvency levy | 603.00 x 0.15% = 0.9045 | 0.90 EUR |
| Accident insurance (example) | 603.00 x 1.50% = 9.045 | 9.05 EUR |
| **Contributions** | | **197.00 EUR** |
| **Total cost** | 603.00 + 197.00 | **800.00 EUR** |

- Without accident insurance, the contributions are 187.95 EUR and the total is 790.95 EUR.
- That is about 32.7% on top of the pay with the example accident rate.

## Worked example: private household

Pay is 603.00 EUR. The household accident rate is fixed at 1.60%.

| Line | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Pension | 603.00 x 5.00% | 30.15 EUR |
| Health | 603.00 x 5.00% | 30.15 EUR |
| Flat-rate tax | 603.00 x 2.00% | 12.06 EUR |
| U1 | 603.00 x 0.80% | 4.82 EUR |
| U2 | 603.00 x 0.22% | 1.33 EUR |
| Insolvency levy | none | 0.00 EUR |
| Accident insurance | 603.00 x 1.60% = 9.648 | 9.65 EUR |
| **Contributions** | | **88.16 EUR** |
| **Total cost** | 603.00 + 88.16 | **691.16 EUR** |

## Cost compared

Total cost on 603.00 EUR of pay (commercial uses the 1.50% example):

- Commercial: 800.00 EUR
- Household: 691.16 EUR
- Pay only: 603.00 EUR
- The household total is 108.84 EUR lower, mainly because pension and health are 5% each instead of 15% and 13%.

## What the worker pays

- The worker pays a pension share unless they opt out: 3.6% with a commercial employer, 13.6% in a private household.
- Below a monthly pay of 175 EUR the pension share is calculated on a minimum base of 175 EUR.

## Limits to keep in mind

- The pay itself must stay within the monthly limit: 603 EUR in 2026, 633 EUR in 2027 (provisional).
- The hourly wage must not fall below the minimum wage: 13.90 EUR in 2026 and 14.60 EUR in 2027.
- Contributions are on top of the pay and do not count toward the limit.

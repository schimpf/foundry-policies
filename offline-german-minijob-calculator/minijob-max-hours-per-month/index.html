Minijob Maximum Hours per Month 2026 and 2027

How many hours you can work in a German Minijob each month at your hourly wage. Formula, worked example and a table for 2026 and 2027.

A Minijob has a monthly earnings limit. The hours you can work depend on your hourly wage. This page shows the formula, a worked example and a table for 2026 and 2027.

The limits

20262027 (provisional)
Monthly earnings limit603 EUR633 EUR
Yearly earnings limit7,236 EUR7,596 EUR
Minimum wage per hour13.90 EUR14.60 EUR
  • The monthly limit is tied to the statutory minimum wage. It is 130 hours at minimum wage divided by 3 (so 3 months at 130 hours is 130 / 3 = 43.33 hours a month), rounded up to the next full euro.
  • The yearly limit is the monthly limit times 12. For 2026 that is 603 x 12 = 7,236 EUR.
  • The 2027 values are provisional until they are officially confirmed.
  • Sources: Minijob-Zentrale (minijob-zentrale.de), section 8 of Book IV of the Social Code (SGB IV), and the Mindestlohnkommission decision on the minimum wage for 2026 and 2027.

Formula

Maximum hours per month = monthly limit / hourly wage

  • The app works in minutes and rounds down, so you never plan past the limit.
  • Minutes = limit in cents x 60 / wage in cents, rounded down.
  • Then convert minutes to hours and minutes.

Worked example

You earn 15.00 EUR per hour in 2026.

  1. Limit: 603.00 EUR.
  2. 603.00 / 15.00 = 40.2 hours.
  3. 0.2 hours x 60 = 12 minutes.
  4. Result: 40 hours 12 minutes per month.

Another example at 13.90 EUR (the 2026 minimum wage):

  1. 603.00 / 13.90 = 43.38 hours.
  2. In minutes: 60,300 x 60 / 1,390 = 2,602.9, rounded down to 2,602 minutes.
  3. Result: 43 hours 22 minutes.

Things that change the picture

  • Yearly limit: what counts is your actual earnings over the calendar year, up to 7,236 EUR in 2026. A month under the limit leaves room for another month.
  • Occasional overruns: the rules allow going over the monthly limit in up to 2 months a year. Each time, the excess may be at most one monthly limit, so that month's earnings reach at most twice the limit. This is meant for unforeseen cases such as covering a sick colleague.
  • Midijob range: above the Minijob limit, up to 2,000 EUR a month, you are in the Midijob range. Social contributions then start for you and work differently. This page does not calculate it.
  • Several Minijobs: the limit applies to all your Minijobs together. The app warns about several Minijobs but does not track them.
  • Pension share: with a commercial employer you pay 3.6% of your pay into pension insurance unless you opt out. In a private household it is 13.6%. See the app for your net pay with and without it.

Check your hours as you go

A fixed number of hours is only a plan, because real shifts run long, wages change and breaks differ. The Offline German Minijob Calculator for Android lets you:

  • Enter your hourly wage and get the maximum hours for 2026 or 2027.
  • Log shifts with start, end and break, including overnight shifts.
  • See the money and hours left this month, updated with every shift.
  • Get a warning before a shift would break the monthly or yearly limit.
  • Track the year total and mark up to 2 occasional overruns.

It works offline and needs no account. Results are estimates, not tax or legal advice.

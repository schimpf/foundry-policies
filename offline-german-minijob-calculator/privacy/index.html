Privacy Policy

Minijob Calculator (Offline German Minijob Calculator), package es.schimpf.offlinegermanminijobcalculator
Effective date: 8 November 2026

Summary

The app works fully offline. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing you enter leaves your device through the app. The developer receives none of your data.

Who is responsible

Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es

What the app stores

Only what you enter yourself, saved in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Shifts: date, start and end time, break and an optional note.
  • Your hourly wage and the date it applies from.
  • Months you mark as an occasional overrun, with the reason you type.
  • Settings: employer type, pension opt-out, accident insurance rate, default break, and whether you dismissed a hint.
  • Your chosen app language (stored by Android).

The app uses this data only to calculate earnings, hours, limits and employer cost and to show them in the app and in the home screen widget. The database is not encrypted; it is protected by Android's normal app sandbox.

What the app does not do

  • No network access: the app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data.
  • No account, login or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash reporting tools.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or other apps' data.
  • No sharing or selling of data to anyone.

Widget

If you add the home screen widget, it shows the amount you have left this month and this year. These amounts are visible to anyone who can see your home screen. Remove the widget if you do not want that.

Backup and device transfer

The app's data is excluded from Google cloud backup. On Android 12 and newer, it can be copied to a new phone by Android's device-to-device transfer, if you choose to use that. On Android 11 and older, the data is not included in any backup.

Deleting your data

You can delete single shifts and wages inside the app. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > Minijob Calculator > Storage > Clear storage) or uninstall the app. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data about the download and installation under its own privacy policy. The developer may see anonymous, aggregated statistics from Google Play Console (such as install counts), which do not identify you.

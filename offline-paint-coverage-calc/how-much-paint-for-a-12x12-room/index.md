# How Much Paint for a 12x12 Room? Gallons by Room Size

> A 12x12 room with 8 ft walls needs about 2 gallons and 1 quart for two coats. Table of paint by room size, with the numbers behind it.

- Web: https://apps.schimpf.es/offline-paint-coverage-calc/how-much-paint-for-a-12x12-room/
- App: Paint Calc: Room Paint Amount, https://apps.schimpf.es/offline-paint-coverage-calc/index.md
A 12 × 12 ft room with 8 ft walls, one door and one window needs about 2 gallons and 1 quart of paint for two coats on the walls. A ceiling in the same room adds about 1 gallon. This page shows the numbers and a table for other room sizes.

## Assumptions

The figures below use these settings. Two coats, 350 sq ft per gallon and 10% waste are the app's starting values, and the door and window are its presets:

- 8 ft ceiling height
- One door (3 × 7 ft = 21 sq ft) and one window (3 × 4 ft = 12 sq ft), so 33 sq ft of openings
- Two coats
- Coverage of 350 sq ft per gallon
- Smooth walls
- 10% waste

If your can states a different coverage, or your walls are textured, the result changes. See the adjustments below.

## The 12 × 12 room, step by step

1. Gross walls: 2 × (12 + 12) × 8 = 384 sq ft
2. Net walls: 384 − 33 = 351 sq ft
3. Paint for two coats: 351 × 2 ÷ 350 = 2.01 gal
4. With 10% waste: 2.01 × 1.10 = 2.21 gal
5. Rounded up to quarts: 9 quarts = 2 gallons and 1 quart

### The ceiling

- Area: 12 × 12 = 144 sq ft
- Two coats: 144 × 2 ÷ 350 = 0.82 gal
- With 10% waste: 0.91 gal
- Rounded up: 4 quarts = 1 gallon

## Adjust for your own room

### Different coverage

Paint needed scales with 1 ÷ coverage. A paint rated at 400 sq ft per gallon needs about 12% less than one rated at 350. Use the figure on your can.

### One coat

Halve the walls figure. For 12 × 12 that is 1.10 gal, which rounds to 1 gallon and 1 quart. One coat is rarely enough when you change color.

### Textured walls

Add 10% for light texture and 20% for heavy texture. For 12 × 12 with heavy texture: 2.01 × 1.20 × 1.10 = 2.65 gal, which rounds to 3 gallons.

### Taller walls

Net wall area grows in step with height. A 9 ft ceiling adds 12.5% to the gross wall area. Run the numbers again, because the table assumes 8 ft walls.

### No doors or windows to subtract

If you skip the openings, 12 × 12 grows to 384 sq ft and 2.41 gal, which rounds to 2 gallons and 2 quarts. Subtracting them saves paint when you have measured them.

## Metric version

Take a 4 × 4 m room with 2.5 m walls, one door (0.9 × 2.1 m) and one window (1.0 × 1.2 m), at 10 m² per liter:

1. Gross walls: 2 × (4 + 4) × 2.5 = 40 m²
2. Net walls: 40 − 1.89 − 1.2 = 36.91 m²
3. Two coats: 36.91 × 2 ÷ 10 = 7.38 L
4. With 10% waste: 8.12 L
5. Rounded up to 0.5 L: 8.5 L, which is one 5 L tin, one 2.5 L tin and one 1 L tin

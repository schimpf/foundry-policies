Privacy Policy

App: Paint Calc (package es.schimpf.offlinepaintcoveragecalc)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

Short version

Paint Calc does not collect your data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not request the internet permission and cannot send your data to me or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Rooms: name, dimensions, doors and windows, coats, coverage, texture and waste.
  • Pro features: project names, client names, paint names, paint prices, an optional labor amount and your business name.
  • Settings: units, currency, default coverage and waste, and which room or project you opened last (used by the home screen widget).
  • A cached flag that remembers whether Pro is unlocked.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. I cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions and network

  • The app has no internet permission and no network access of its own.
  • The only permission it declares is the Google Play Billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING), used for the optional Pro purchase.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment and purchase record under its own privacy policy. I do not receive your payment details or your identity. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your account.

Sharing and PDF quotes

When you tap Share or Export PDF, the app hands the text or PDF to the app you choose in the Android share sheet. After that, the receiving app's policy applies. Temporary PDF files are kept in the app's cache and are removed on the next app start.

Home screen widget

The widget shows the paint total of the last room or project you opened. It shows the project name, never the client name. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it, so remove the widget if that matters to you.

Third parties

No analytics, advertising or tracking services are included. No data is sold or shared.

Children

The app is a general utility. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. You control all data in the app and can delete rooms and projects inside it, or remove everything by uninstalling or clearing the app's storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

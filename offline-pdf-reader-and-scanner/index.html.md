## About

A fast, private PDF reader and document scanner. It works without a connection, shows no ads and has no internet permission, so your files never leave your phone unless you share or export them.

## Features

### Read pdfs

- Open PDFs from your phone, cloud apps or "Open with". No access to all your files needed.
- Smooth vertical scrolling at full width, on phones and tablets.
- Picks up at the page where you stopped.
- Table of contents for PDFs that have one.
- Search text in PDFs that already have a text layer.
- Night pages: invert page colours for reading in the dark. The app itself follows your light or dark system setting.
- Password-protected PDFs on Android 15 and newer.
- Recent files list that you can clear at any time.

### Scan documents

- Edge detection and cropping with the Google ML Kit document scanner.
- Unlimited scans, no watermark.
- Scans are saved as PDF in a private library on your phone.
- Search, sort and rename your scans, with page thumbnails.
- Add and delete pages of a saved scan.
- Export to any folder or share as PDF.

### Pro: one-time purchase

- Searchable scans: text recognition runs on your phone after every scan, and works on image-only PDFs too.
- Highlight text with standard PDF highlights that other readers show.
- Sign documents with a drawn signature. Your signature is encrypted with a key that never leaves the phone.
- Edits are saved as a copy in your library. Your original file is never changed.
- No subscription, no trial, no account.

### Private by design

- No internet permission. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked.
- No account, no analytics.
- The library is excluded from backups.
- No ads.

### Home screen widget

Your last three scans or opened PDFs and a one-tap Scan button.

Available in English, German and Spanish.

Scanning and Pro need Google Play services. Reading works without them. Text recognition supports the Latin alphabet.

## Questions and answers

### Does the app work offline?

Yes. The app has no internet permission, so reading, scanning and text recognition all run on your phone.

### Where are my files stored?

Scans and edited copies stay in a private library on your phone, which is excluded from Android backups. A file leaves the phone only when you share or export it.

### What can I do for free?

Reading, search in PDFs that already have text, unlimited scanning without a watermark, a library with search and sort, page editing, export and share.

### What needs the Pro purchase?

Making scans searchable (OCR), highlighting text and signing with a drawn signature. Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, with no subscription or account.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 and newer. Password-protected PDFs open on Android 15 and newer, and older versions show a message instead.

### Does it change my original PDF?

No. Highlights, signatures and text recognition on an imported PDF are saved as a copy in your library. Scans from the app are replaced by their searchable version.

Privacy policy

PDF Reader & Scanner (Android, package es.schimpf.offlinepdfreaderandscanner)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf · Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective date: 8 November 2026

  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything over the network itself.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Your documents stay on your device unless you share or export them yourself.
  • The developer never receives any of your data.

1. Data the app stores on your device

All of the following is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

  • Scans and library copies: PDF files you scan, and copies created when you run text recognition, highlight or sign a document.
  • Library details: document names you choose, dates, page counts, file sizes, small page thumbnails and the last page you read.
  • Recent files: for PDFs you open from elsewhere, the app remembers a reference to the file (a content URI), its name, a thumbnail, the last page you read and when you opened it. It keeps at most 50 entries. The original file is only read, never changed.
  • Signature: if you draw one (Pro), it is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key held in the Android Keystore, which cannot be exported from the device.
  • Settings: text recognition on or off, night pages, sort order.
  • Pro status: whether Pro is unlocked, a one-way hash of the Google Play purchase token and the time it was last checked.

App data is excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to Google Drive or a new phone.

If you add the app's home screen widget, it shows the names of your three most recent documents on your home screen.

2. Permissions

  • No internet or network-state permission. These are removed from the app on purpose.
  • No storage or "all files" permission. The app only sees files you pick in the Android file picker or open with "Open with".
  • No camera permission. The camera is used by the Google document scanner (see section 3), not by the app.
  • Notifications: used only to show the progress of text recognition on longer documents.
  • Foreground service: lets text recognition keep running while the progress notification is shown.
  • Google Play billing: needed to buy Pro.

3. Google services the app uses

  • Document scanner (Google ML Kit, part of Google Play services): when you tap Scan, Google Play services opens the camera, detects page edges and hands the result to the app as a PDF. The scanner runs in Google Play services on your device, not in this app.
  • Text recognition (Google ML Kit, bundled model, Pro): runs inside the app on your device. Because the app has no internet permission, page images and recognized text cannot be sent anywhere by the app.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro, the purchase is handled by Google Play. The app checks the purchase signature on the device; there is no developer server.

Google Play services and the Google Play Store are separate apps with their own network access. Their handling of data is covered by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Sharing and export

A document leaves the app only when you choose Share or Export. Share hands a temporary, read-only copy to the app you pick; the temporary copy is deleted the next time the app starts. Export writes the file to a folder you choose. What happens next depends on the app or location you chose.

5. What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data, usage data, device identifiers or documents.

6. Deleting your data

  • Delete a document from the library with its menu.
  • Remove recent files one by one or with "Clear recent files" in Settings. This also gives up the app's access to those files.
  • Replace your signature by drawing a new one.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything the app stored. Files you exported or shared are not affected.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

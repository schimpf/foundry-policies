How to Sign a PDF on Android With Your Finger

Many forms still need a signature, and printing them is a detour. You can draw your signature on the phone screen and place it on the PDF. This page shows the steps and explains what a drawn signature is.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet (Android 8.0 or newer for this app).
  • The PDF you want to sign.
  • An app that supports drawn signatures. Offline PDF reader and scanner does this in Pro, a one-time purchase.

Step 1: draw your signature once

  1. Open Settings and tap Signature.
  2. Sign with your finger or a stylus. Turn the phone sideways for more room.
  3. Keep your signature above the line.
  4. Use Undo stroke to remove the last stroke or Clear to start again.
  5. Tap Save signature.

The app keeps one signature. It is encrypted with a key from the Android Keystore that never leaves the phone, and it is excluded from backups. To change it, draw a new one. Signatures already placed in PDFs stay as they were.

Step 2: place it on the PDF

  1. Open the PDF in the reader.
  2. Tap Add signature in the reader tools.
  3. Drag the signature to the signature line.
  4. Pinch to resize it.
  5. Tap Done.

The app tells you it saved the result as a copy in your library. Your original file is never touched.

Step 3: share or export

  • Use Share to send the signed copy by email or a messenger.
  • Use Export to save it to a folder you choose.

Both send a PDF. The signature is stored in the file as a standard PDF stamp annotation.

Remove or redo a signature

  • Tap a placed signature to remove it.
  • Add it again somewhere else if it was in the wrong place.
  • Editing the same file again overwrites the signed copy, so there is no pile of versions.

Tips for a clean signature

  • Draw bigger than you think. You can shrink it later, and large strokes look smoother.
  • Use a stylus if you have one.
  • Place it so it sits on the line without covering printed text.
  • Zoom in before you drop it to check the position.

Is a drawn signature valid?

It depends on the document and the country. This is general information, not legal advice.

  • EU. Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS), Article 25(1), says an electronic signature cannot be denied legal effect only because it is in electronic form. A drawn image is a simple electronic signature. Stronger kinds, such as a qualified electronic signature, need a certified provider and are not what this app creates.
  • United States. The ESIGN Act (15 U.S.C. § 7001, 2000) says a signature cannot be denied legal effect only because it is electronic. Some documents are excluded, such as wills and certain court or utility notices.
  • Other countries and special forms. Rules differ. Notary, property and government forms may require a handwritten signature or a certified electronic one.

If the other side asks for a particular kind of signature, ask them before you sign.

What a drawn signature does not do

  • It does not lock the document. Someone can still edit the PDF afterward.
  • It does not prove who signed. It is an image of your hand-drawn mark.
  • It does not carry a certificate or a timestamp.

For contracts where proof matters, add a line to your email that confirms you agree to the signed copy.

Questions

Does the signature leave my phone?

No. The app has no internet permission. Only a file you share or export leaves the phone.

Can I type my name instead?

No. Signatures are drawn only.

Can I sign a scan?

Yes. Scan the paper with the app, save it, then open it and add your signature. Highlighting text needs a text layer, but placing a signature does not.

Does it work without Google Play services?

Reading works. Pro cannot be bought without them.

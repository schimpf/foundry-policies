## About

Plumber Invoice is a quote and invoice app for solo plumbers. You write the quote at the job, the customer signs with a finger, and one tap turns it into an invoice. Everything works without signal, so basements and crawlspaces are no problem.

## Features

### Quotes

Add lines from the built-in price book or type your own. The price book has about 120 common plumbing items in eight categories: pipe, fittings, valves, water heaters, fixtures, drains, gas and labor. Items come without prices. You enter your cost and markup once, and the app reuses them on every plumbing quote. Set the sales tax and the deposit percentage on each quote. Buttons and quantity steppers are large, so you can use them with gloves on. Every change saves on the device as you type, and each edit screen shows "Saved on device".

### Signature

The customer signs on a full-screen pad in portrait or landscape. The signer name and the time go on the quote. A signed quote is locked until you clear the signature.

### Invoices and payments

Create the invoice from a signed quote with one tap. The quote lines are copied over. Record cash, check or other payments with date and reference, including the deposit, and the invoice shows the balance due. The app blocks a payment larger than the balance. Quotes are numbered Q-0001, invoices INV-0001. You can change both prefixes.

### Pdf and sending

The app builds the PDF on your phone. Send it by SMS, WhatsApp, email or any app in the Android share sheet. With no signal, the document waits in the Outbox, and a notification tells you when signal is back. You tap Send yourself.

### Your data

There is no account and no server. The app has no internet permission. The database, photos and signatures are encrypted on the device. You can turn on an app lock with fingerprint, face or device PIN. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase you choose and go to a file or Google Drive. If you lose the passphrase, the backup cannot be opened.

### Phone, tablet and widget

Phones and tablets have their own layouts. The theme follows your system setting. The home screen widget shows your open balance and the number of unpaid invoices, with a New quote button. It hides amounts while the app lock is on.

### Free and pro

Free: unlimited jobs, quotes and invoices. PDFs carry a watermark. Pro is a one-time purchase in Google Play. It adds your logo, the customer's signature image and job photos to the PDF, and removes the watermark. There is no subscription.

Made for US plumbers: US dollars and one combined sales tax rate per document. Check your state's rules for what an invoice must show, such as your contractor license number. This plumber invoice app does not send anything over the internet by itself.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It makes plumbing quotes and invoices on your phone or tablet. The customer signs the quote with a finger, one tap turns it into an invoice, and you record payments and send the PDF by SMS, WhatsApp or email.

### Does it work without signal?

Yes. Quotes, signatures, invoices and PDFs all work offline. A document you cannot send yet waits in the Outbox, and you get a notification when signal is back.

### Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, encrypted. There is no account and no server, and the app has no internet permission. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase you choose and go to a file or Google Drive, and a lost passphrase cannot be recovered.

### What does the app not do?

It does not take card payments, sync between devices or support crews. It also has no accounting links, no VAT and no other currencies than US dollars.

### Do I have to pay for anything?

Jobs, quotes and invoices are unlimited for free, but PDFs carry a watermark. A one-time Pro purchase in Google Play adds your logo, the customer's signature image and job photos to the PDF and removes the watermark. There is no subscription.

### Which Android versions does it run on?

Android 10 and newer, on phones and tablets. The app is in English and made for US plumbers.

Privacy Policy

Pipe Sizing (package es.schimpf.offlineplumbingpipesizing)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Effective: 2026-11-13

Summary

  • The app does not collect, send or share any personal data.
  • The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
  • The app does not request the internet permission.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps this data in a private database that only the app can read:

  • Your last calculation (inputs and result). The home-screen widget shows it.
  • Saved jobs and their segments, if you use that feature: job names, segment names, loads, materials, velocity limits and lengths. Job names may contain text you type yourself, such as a site name. Enter only what you are comfortable keeping on your phone.
  • Whether you accepted the disclaimer, the bundled data version last seen, and whether the paid features are unlocked, including the purchase token Google Play gave the app for that unlock.

This data never leaves your device through the app. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also delete a job or a segment in the app, or clear the app's storage in Android settings.

Permissions and network use

  • The app requests only the Google Play Billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING), used for the in-app purchase.
  • It does not request internet, network state, location, camera, contacts, storage, microphone or notification permissions.
  • The app's own code makes no network connections.

Purchases

Some features need an in-app purchase. The purchase is made and processed by Google Play on your device. Google handles your payment details and receipt under its own Privacy Policy. The developer receives no payment details or personal data from the app. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase and keeps the result on your device so the paid features keep working without a connection.

Reviewer code

The app can unlock the paid features with a code given to Google Play reviewers. The code is checked on the device against a stored hash. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Third parties and sharing

The developer does not share data with anyone, because the developer does not receive any. The only third-party component that handles data is Google Play and its Billing Library, described above.

Children

The app is a work tool for plumbing professionals and students. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

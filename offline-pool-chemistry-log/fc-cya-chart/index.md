# FC/CYA Chart for Pool Chlorine Levels

> The FC/CYA chart as a table: minimum, target, SLAM and mustard chlorine levels for every CYA from 0 to 100 ppm, with a worked example.

- Web: https://apps.schimpf.es/offline-pool-chemistry-log/fc-cya-chart/
- App: Pool Chem Log: Water Tests, https://apps.schimpf.es/offline-pool-chemistry-log/index.md
The right chlorine level for your pool depends on how much stabilizer (cyanuric acid, CYA) is in the water. CYA protects chlorine from sunlight, but it also slows chlorine down. More CYA means you need more FC to get the same sanitizing power. The FC/CYA chart gives you the numbers.

FC is free chlorine, the active chlorine your test kit reads in the water. Both FC and CYA are in ppm (parts per million).

## FC/CYA chart for chlorine pools

Find your CYA in the first column. Read across for your FC levels.

| CYA (ppm) | Minimum FC | Target FC | SLAM FC | Mustard algae FC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 1 to 3 | 10 | 12 |
| 10 | 1 | 2 to 4 | 10 | 12 |
| 20 | 2 | 3 to 5 | 10 | 12 |
| 30 | 2 | 4 to 6 | 12 | 15 |
| 40 | 3 | 5 to 7 | 16 | 20 |
| 50 | 4 | 6 to 8 | 20 | 24 |
| 60 | 5 | 7 to 9 | 24 | 29 |
| 70 | 5 | 8 to 10 | 28 | 34 |
| 80 | 6 | 9 to 11 | 31 | 38 |
| 90 | 7 | 10 to 12 | 35 | 42 |
| 100 | 8 | 11 to 13 | 39 | 47 |

Source: the "Chlorine / CYA chart" published by Trouble Free Pool (TFP), troublefreepool.com. Check the current chart there if you want the original.

## What each column means

- **Minimum FC:** the lowest level you should let the pool reach. Below this, algae can start to grow.
- **Target FC:** the band to aim for in normal use. Keep FC inside it between tests.
- **SLAM FC:** the level used when you shock the pool to clear algae or high combined chlorine (CC). SLAM stands for Shock Level And Maintain.
- **Mustard algae FC:** a higher level in TFP's chart for mustard algae, which is harder to kill.

## The rule of thumb behind the chart

The chart follows simple percentages of your CYA:

- Minimum FC is about 7.5% of CYA.
- Target FC is about 11.5% of CYA.
- SLAM FC is about 40% of CYA.

At CYA 40, that gives 3 ppm minimum, about 5 ppm target and 16 ppm SLAM. The table above matches those numbers.

## Salt water (SWG) pools

A salt chlorine generator adds chlorine all day, so TFP publishes a lower FC range for SWG pools at CYA 50 and above. Below CYA 50 the regular chart applies.

| CYA (ppm) | Minimum FC | Target FC |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | 2 | 3 to 5 |
| 60 | 3 | 4 to 6 |
| 70 | 3 | 4 to 6 |
| 80 | 4 | 5 to 7 |
| 90 | 4 | 5 to 7 |
| 100 | 5 | 6 to 8 |

SLAM levels stay the same as in the regular chart. TFP's SWG pools typically run CYA 70 to 80.

## Worked example

Your pool tests at CYA 40 and FC 2.

1. Look up CYA 40 in the table. Minimum FC is 3, target is 5 to 7.
2. FC 2 is below the minimum. Raise it now.
3. Aim for the middle of the target band, 6 ppm. You need to add 4 ppm.
4. Add the chlorine product, wait for it to mix, and retest before adding more.

Now take a pool at FC 6 and CYA 80. The row for CYA 80 shows a minimum of 6 and a target of 9 to 11. FC 6 sits right at the minimum and below the target band. High CYA is the cause. Lowering CYA means replacing some of the water, since no product removes it.

## What if your CYA is between two rows?

Use the nearest row. CYA 44 uses the 40 row, and CYA 46 uses the 50 row. Above 100, use the 100 row. Most test kits cannot read CYA to better than about 10 ppm, so a finer chart would not help.

## Other targets that go with it

- **CC (combined chlorine):** 0 to 0.5 ppm. Above 0.5 means organic matter in the water, and a SLAM is the usual answer.
- **CYA:** TFP guidance is 30 to 50 ppm for chlorine pools, 70 to 80 ppm for SWG pools, and 30 to 40 ppm for spas.
- **pH:** 7.2 to 7.8, aiming near 7.5.

## Test, then change, then test again

Doses are estimates. Retest before you add more. Never mix chemicals, wear protection, and follow the product label. If a label and a chart disagree, follow the label.

## Get the chart and the dose in one place

Pool Chem Log is an Android app that works offline. You enter your test results, and it picks the right row of the chart from your CYA, shows what is out of range, and calculates the dose for the chlorine product you actually use. It splits big changes into several additions with a wait time between them. For SWG pools it suggests a change to the cell output. The app targets follow TFP guidance.

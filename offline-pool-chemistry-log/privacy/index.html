Privacy Policy: Pool Chem Log

Effective 2026-11-13. App: Pool Chem Log (package es.schimpf.offlinepoolchemistrylog). Developer: Gerrit Schimpf. Contact: apps@schimpf.es.

Short version

The app does not collect your data. It has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It cannot connect to the internet. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app stores the following on your device only, in its private app storage:

  • Your pools and spas: name, type, volume, surface and sanitizer.
  • Water test results, doses you marked as added, maintenance entries and SLAM records.
  • Your settings, custom products and reminder schedules.
  • Whether Pro is unlocked on this device.

This data is not sent to the developer or to any third party by the app. The developer cannot see it.

Network use

The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it has no way to send data over the network. The only outside service it talks to is the Google Play Store app on your phone, through the Android Play Billing library. This is used to buy and check the Pro unlock. Google handles the payment and the purchase record under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show the test and maintenance reminders you set up. Reminders are scheduled on the device. You are asked only when you turn on a reminder.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) and related background-work permissions added by the Android WorkManager library: to restore scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): for the Pro unlock through Google Play.

The app does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or file storage permissions.

Backups and files you export

  • Android backup: the app allows Android's automatic backup and device transfer of its database. This is run by Android and Google under your device and Google account settings. Cloud backup is used only when your device supports encrypted backup. The developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in Android settings.
  • Manual backup: you can export your data as a JSON file, and as CSV files with Pro. You choose where the file is saved. The JSON file is plain text and not encrypted, and the app warns you before writing it. Keep it somewhere private. The app does not upload these files.
  • Import: you can load a backup file you pick. The app checks the file and rejects it if it is not valid.

Home screen widget

The optional widget shows your latest readings from the on-device data. It sends nothing anywhere.

Sharing and selling

The app does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone, because it never has access to it.

Deleting your data

You can delete tests, doses and maintenance entries in the app, or clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because no data is held by the developer, there is nothing to delete on a server. Files you exported and Android cloud backups are under your control; delete them yourself if you no longer want them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Safety notice

Dose results are estimates. Always retest, follow the product label and never mix chemicals. This notice is not part of data handling but is repeated in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

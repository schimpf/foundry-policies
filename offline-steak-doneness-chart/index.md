## About

Know exactly when to take your meat off the heat.

Pick the meat, the cut and how you like it done. You get three numbers in big type:

- Pull temp: when to take it off the heat - Final temp: where it ends up after carryover - Rest time: how long to let it sit before slicing

Then tap one button to start the rest timer.

## Features

### Safety warnings

Chef-style temps, such as a medium rare burger or a juicy chicken breast, can be below the USDA safe minimum. The app still shows them, but marks them with a clear warning and shows the USDA minimum next to them.

### Make it yours

Like your ribeye a little rarer? Edit the temps for any built-in cut. Add your own cuts, like picanha or pulled pork shoulder. Reset any edit to the default with one tap. Custom temps get the same USDA warning.

### One simple timer

Rest presets from 3 to 20 minutes and flip presets from 1 to 4 minutes. An alarm sounds when it ends, and the screen stays on while it counts down. Leaving the app? Tap Set in Clock app and your phone's Clock app takes over the countdown.

### Home-screen widget

Your last lookup stays on your home screen: cut, doneness, pull temp and final temp. Tap it to jump back in.

### Built for the grill

- Big text and large buttons for greasy fingers
- °F or °C, remembered
- Light and dark theme that follows your phone
- Phone and tablet layouts; on tablets the chart and timer sit side by side
- Screen reader support

### Privacy

- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot go online
- No account, no tracking, no analytics
- Your custom temps are included in your Android backup. Nothing else leaves the device.

### Purchase

The app is a paid download. All features are included after purchase. There are no in-app purchases.

Covers beef, pork, chicken, lamb and fish. Safe minimums are based on USDA FSIS. Temperatures are guidance only; always use a calibrated thermometer.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

You pick the meat, the cut and the doneness, and it shows the pull temp, the final temp after carryover and the rest time. It covers beef, pork, chicken, lamb and fish, and you can edit any temp or add your own cuts.

### What does the app not do?

It does not connect to Bluetooth thermometers, give cooking times by thickness, list sous-vide temps or include recipes. The timer runs one countdown at a time.

### Does it work without internet, and where does my data stay?

It works fully offline because the app has no internet permission. Your custom temps and settings stay on your phone, and the custom temps are included in your Android backup. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

### Do I need to buy anything besides the app?

No. The app is a paid download, every feature is included and there are no in-app purchases.

### Which Android versions does it support?

Android 10 and newer, on phones and tablets. On tablets the chart and the timer sit side by side.

### Will the timer alert me if I leave the app?

The timer sounds its alarm only while the app is open. Tap Set in Clock app to hand the countdown to your phone's Clock app, which alerts you with the app closed.

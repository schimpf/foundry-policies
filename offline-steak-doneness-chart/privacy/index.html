Privacy Policy

App: Steak Doneness Chart & Timer (package es.schimpf.offlinesteakdonenesschart)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

Summary

The app does not collect, send or share any data. It has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting.

What is stored on your device

  • Your custom and edited temperatures (meat, cut name, doneness label, temps, rest time), in a private app database.
  • Your settings: the °F/°C choice and your last lookup (used by the home-screen widget).
  • The state of a running timer, so it can be restored.

This data stays in the app's private storage. I cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to alert you when a rest or flip timer ends. Asked when you start a timer. You can refuse; the app still works, but alerts will not sound when it is closed.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep the timer running and alert you with the app closed or the screen off. No data is involved.
  • No internet, network state, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Android backup

Your custom and edited temperatures are included in Android's backup and device transfer, if you have them turned on. Google handles that backup under its own terms and privacy policy. Settings and timer state are excluded. I do not receive any backup data.

Third parties

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries and shares nothing with anyone.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deletion

Since I hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on my side. You can delete your custom temps inside the app (My Temps) or remove all data by clearing app storage or uninstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

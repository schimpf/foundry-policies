## About

Field Forms is the paperwork app for solo tradespeople and small crews: electricians, plumbers, heating engineers and general builders. Fill in job forms, take photos, get the client's signature and send a clean PDF from your phone. It works without signal and without an account.

## Features

### Jobs and forms

- Jobs with a client, a site address, and as many documents and photos as you need
- Job sheets, service reports and inspection checklists (pass, fail or N/A, plus your own items)
- A job can hold several forms of the same kind
- Mark a job done to move it to the archive, and search by title, client, address or document number

### Quotes and invoices

- Line items with quantity and unit price, tax rate and totals
- Discounts and deposits as negative lines
- Automatic numbering such as QUO-0001 and INV-0001, with prefixes you choose
- A quote signed by the client counts as accepted and converts to an invoice in one tap
- Due dates, paid and unpaid tracking, and an overdue marker

### Photos and signatures

- Add photos from your camera app or gallery, with captions
- Photos are resized, and location and camera details are removed
- The client signs on the screen
- Save your own signature once and reuse it

### Pdf and sharing

- Your business name, address, phone, email and tax ID on every document
- Photos with captions and times added to job sheets, reports and checklists
- A4 or Letter page size
- Share by email, WhatsApp or SMS from the share sheet. The client's email is filled in for you.
- Signed or shared documents become read-only. Duplicate one to make changes.

### Clients

- A reusable client list with call, email and map shortcuts
- Erase a client together with all their jobs, documents and photos in one step

### Privacy and backup

- No account, no server and no analytics. Your data stays on your phone.
- Optional PIN or fingerprint lock
- Export a backup to Files or Google Drive, with optional AES-256 password protection, and restore it later

### Home screen widget

Shows your unpaid and overdue invoice totals and a button to start a new job. Amounts are hidden while the app lock is on.

### Phones and tablets

Large buttons for use on site. On a tablet, the job list and job details sit side by side. Light and dark themes follow your phone's setting. The app is in English.

### Pro upgrade

All features work without paying, except two. PDFs made without Pro carry a small watermark, and adding your logo to PDFs needs Pro. Pro is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play and can be restored on the same Google account.

The forms are general-purpose job records. They are not certified forms such as gas safety records or electrical installation condition reports.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It handles the paperwork for a job: job sheets, service reports, inspection checklists, quotes and invoices, with photos and client signatures, shared as PDF. It also tracks due dates and shows an overdue marker on unpaid invoices.

### What does it not do?

It has no certified forms such as gas safety records or electrical installation condition reports, no cloud sync, no payment processing and no scheduling. The app is in English only.

### Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. There is no account, no server and no analytics. You can export a backup through the Android file picker (for example to Google Drive), with an optional AES-256 password.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes. Creating jobs, forms, photos, signatures and PDFs works without signal. You only need a connection to send a PDF by email or WhatsApp, or to buy Pro.

### Do I have to pay for anything?

Every feature works for free. PDFs made without Pro carry a watermark, and adding your logo to PDFs needs Pro. Pro is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play and can be restored on the same Google account.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API level 26) and newer, on phones and tablets.

Privacy Policy

App: Field Forms (Offline Trades Field Forms), package es.schimpf.offlinetradesfieldforms
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 13 November 2026

Short version

The app keeps your data on your phone. It has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. I do not receive any of your data.

What the app stores

Everything you enter is stored on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • Clients (name, address, phone, email), jobs, forms, checklists, quotes and invoices.
  • Photos you add to a job and their captions. Photos are resized and re-saved without location and camera data. Only the time the photo was taken is kept.
  • Signatures you draw, and the settings you choose (business details, tax rate, currency, number prefixes, page size).
  • If you turn on the app lock: a salted hash of your PIN. The PIN itself is not stored.

Android's own device encryption protects this storage. Automatic Android and Google cloud backup of the app is switched off.

Network use

The app does not request the Internet permission and its own code makes no network connections. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

Permissions

  • Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC): only for the optional app lock. The fingerprint or face check is done by Android. The app never sees your biometric data.
  • Google Play Billing: for the optional Pro purchase.
  • No camera, storage, location, contacts or microphone permission. To take a photo the app opens your phone's camera app, and to choose a photo it opens the system photo picker. The app only receives the picture you select.

Google Play purchases

The Pro purchase is handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment and purchase details under Google's Privacy Policy. The app only learns whether Pro is active and stores that status on the device. I do not see your payment details.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you start it:

  • Sharing a PDF: the app opens the Android share sheet. The PDF goes to the app you pick (email, WhatsApp, SMS and so on) and is then handled by that app under its own policy. The client's email is pre-filled if you saved one.
  • Backup: you choose where the backup file is saved (for example Files or Google Drive). You can protect it with a password (AES-256). Without a password the file is not encrypted.
  • Calling, emailing or opening a map from a client's details opens the matching app on your phone.

Home screen widget

The optional widget shows unpaid and overdue invoice totals from data on your phone. While the app lock is on, amounts are hidden.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, you can erase a client together with all their jobs, documents and photos.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its data from the device.
  • Backup files you saved and PDFs you shared are outside the app, so delete those yourself where you stored or sent them.

Your data and others' data

Because I do not receive or hold your data, I cannot access, correct or delete it for you. You control it fully inside the app. You are responsible for the client details you store, including any legal duties that apply to your business.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

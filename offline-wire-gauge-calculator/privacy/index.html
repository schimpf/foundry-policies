Privacy Policy

App: Wire Gauge Calculator (package es.schimpf.offlinewiregaugecalculator)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

Summary

The app does not collect your data. It has no network access, no account, no ads and no analytics. The developer receives nothing from the app.

Network and permissions

  • The app requests no Android permissions. It does not have the INTERNET permission and cannot send data over the network.
  • It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or device identifiers.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code, and no third-party services.

What is stored on your device

  • Settings: NEC edition, units, voltage-drop target, power factor, raceway type, the 125 % continuous-load default, and whether you accepted the first-launch notice.
  • History: your last 10 calculations. They contain numbers and choices (for example load, length, wire material) and the result text. No names, free text or personal details are stored.

This data lives in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it. The home-screen widget shows your last 3 results from this same history.

Sharing

The app shares nothing by itself. If you tap Share on a result, the app hands that result as plain text to the app you choose in the Android share sheet. What that app does with the text is governed by that app's own policy.

Android backup

The app allows Android backup for the settings and history only. If you have Google backup turned on for your device, Android may store these in your Google account and restore them on a new device. This is handled by Android and Google under your device settings and Google's privacy policy, not by the developer. You can turn it off in your Android system settings.

Deleting your data

  • Clear history in the app: History tab, then the clear button.
  • Remove everything: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this page with a new effective date.

# 101 Okey Scoring Rules: Points Table and Example

> How 101 Okey scoring works: finish bonuses, tile sums, the no-open penalty, wrong okey discards, a worked round and a round calculator.

- Web: https://apps.schimpf.es/okey-score-counter/101-okey-scoring-rules/
- App: Okey Score Counter: 101 Okey, https://apps.schimpf.es/okey-score-counter/index.md
101 Okey is scored over many rounds. The player with the lowest total leads. This page gives the usual point values, a table you can copy, a worked round and a small calculator.

The values below are the defaults used by Okey Score Counter. Many tables play with house rules, so agree on the numbers before the first round.

## 101 Okey points table

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Finish normally | -101 |
| Finish with the okey tile | -202 |
| Finish from hand | -202 |
| Did not open (no melds shown) | +202 |
| Wrong okey or joker discard | +101 each |
| Everyone else who opened | Sum of the tiles left in their hand |

Negative points are good. A finish takes points off the finisher's total. Everyone else adds to theirs.

## How one round is scored

1. Find out who finished. At most one player finishes a round.
2. The finisher gets the finish value for how they finished: -101, -202 for okey, or -202 from hand.
3. Each other player who opened scores the sum of the tiles still in their hand.
4. Each other player who never opened scores 202 instead of their tile sum.
5. Add 101 for every wrong okey or joker discard a player made.
6. Add or subtract any manual adjustment your table agreed on.
7. Add the round to each player's running total.

The finisher's own tile sum is not counted. It is zero by definition, because they have no tiles left.

### Wrong okey discards

A wrong discard is a penalty on top of the round score. It applies to any player, including the finisher. If a player makes two wrong discards in one round, add 101 twice.

## Worked example

Four players, default values. Dana finishes normally.

| Player | Situation | Calculation | Round points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dana | Finished normally | -101 | -101 |
| Emre | Opened, 45 in hand | 45 | 45 |
| Ayşe | Never opened | 202 | 202 |
| Can | Opened, 60 in hand, one wrong okey discard | 60 + 101 | 161 |

After this round Dana has -101, Emre 45, Ayşe 202 and Can 161. Dana leads.

In the next round Emre finishes with the okey tile. He scores -202 and his total becomes 45 - 202 = -157. He now leads.

## Paired play (eşli)

In paired mode four players sit in two teams. Partners sit opposite each other: seats 1 and 3 against seats 2 and 4. Each player is scored as above. The team total is the sum of both partners' totals, and the lower team total leads.

## Equal totals

If two players or teams have the same total, they share the same rank. Both are shown as leading if they tie for the lowest total.

## Calculator

An interactive calculator is on the web page: https://apps.schimpf.es/okey-score-counter/101-okey-scoring-rules/

The calculator uses the same rules as the app. Only one finisher counts per round. If you pick two, the first one is used. Tile sums and point values are limited to the range -999 to 999 per entry, and wrong discards to 9 per player per round.

## Changing the values

Some groups use other numbers. Common changes are a different no-open penalty or a smaller finish bonus. Edit the values in the calculator above, or set them once in the app.

## Questions

### Who wins a game of 101 Okey?

The player or team with the lowest total at the end of the agreed number of rounds, or when a target is reached.

### Why are finish points negative?

Because lower is better. A negative finish value lowers the finisher's total.

### What if nobody can finish?

Agree on this before the game. If you mark no finisher, the app scores every player by tile sum (or the no-open penalty), so nobody gets a finish bonus.

### Is the no-open penalty always 202?

It is the default in the app. Some tables use a different value, so check your house rules.

## About

Keep score at the Okey table without paper. Okey Score Counter scores 101 Okey and standard Okey for 2 to 4 players, individually or in pairs (eşli). The app is available in English and Turkish.

## Features

### Scoring

- Choose 101 Okey or Okey, individual or paired play.
- Enter each round: the remaining tile sum and who finished.
- One-tap options for finishing with okey, finishing from hand, no open and wrong okey or joker discard.
- A live preview shows the round before you save it.
- The lowest total leads, with a leader marker on the scoreboard.

### Fix mistakes

- Edit any past round and all totals recalculate.
- Undo a round, and restore it if you change your mind.
- Change the penalty values for a match and every round recalculates.
- Default rules can be edited. Existing matches keep their own rules.

### History and stats

- Every match is kept in history, with search and a filter by game.
- Player stats show matches, wins, win rate by game, average per round, best match, finishes and wrong okeys.

### Share

- Share the scoreboard as text or as an image.
- You choose what is included: the round table, and real names or Player 1, 2 and so on.

### Widget

- A home-screen widget shows the totals of your current match. Tap it to open the scoreboard.

### Privacy

- No account, no analytics, no tracking.
- The app has no internet permission. Your data stays on your phone.
- The app is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.
- You can delete one match, or all data, in the app.

### Comfort

- Light and dark theme, following your system setting.
- Layouts for phones and tablets.
- Screen reader labels and large touch targets.

### Purchase

The app is a paid download on Google Play. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, and every feature above is included.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It scores 101 Okey and standard Okey for 2 to 4 players, individually or in pairs (eşli). You enter each round, edit or undo any past round, and view match history and player stats.

### What does it not do?

It does not offer online play, accounts, cloud backup or a tile-count calculator. You enter the remaining tile sum yourself.

### Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no analytics and is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. You can delete one match or all data in the app.

### Do I need to buy anything inside the app?

No. The app is a paid download on Google Play with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, and every feature is included.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer, on phones and tablets.

### Which languages does the app have?

English and Turkish. You can switch the language in the app settings.

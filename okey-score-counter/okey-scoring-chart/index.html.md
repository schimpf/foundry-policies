# Okey Scoring Chart: Points for Standard Okey

> A scoring chart for standard Okey with finish bonuses, loser penalties, wrong okey discards, a worked four-player example and pair scoring.

- Web: https://apps.schimpf.es/okey-score-counter/okey-scoring-chart/
- App: Okey Score Counter: 101 Okey, https://apps.schimpf.es/okey-score-counter/index.md
In standard Okey the winner of each round takes a small bonus and the other players take a penalty. As in 101 Okey, the lowest total leads. This page gives a chart of the usual values, shows how a round adds up and explains scoring in pairs.

The numbers are the defaults in Okey Score Counter. Groups often agree on their own values, so settle them before you start.

## Standard Okey scoring chart

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Finish normally | -2 |
| Finish with the okey tile | -4 |
| Finish from hand | -4 |
| Every other player, after a normal finish | +2 |
| Every other player, after a finish with okey | +4 |
| Wrong okey or joker discard | +2 each |

Negative points are good. The finisher's total goes down. Everyone else's goes up.

## How a round is scored

1. Mark who finished the round.
2. The finisher gets -2, or -4 if they finished with the okey tile or from hand.
3. Every other player gets +2 after a normal finish or from-hand finish.
4. After a finish with okey, every other player gets the loser penalty doubled, so +4.
5. Add +2 for each wrong okey or joker discard the player made.
6. Add the round to the running totals.

If nobody finished, nobody scores a base value. Only wrong discards and manual adjustments count.

### Note on finishing from hand

Finishing from hand pays the finisher -4, the same as finishing with okey. The other players still pay the single penalty of +2, because only a finish with the okey tile doubles it.

## Worked example

Four players with the default values.

Round 1. Dana finishes normally. Emre made one wrong okey discard.

| Player | Calculation | Round points |
|---|---|---|
| Dana | -2 | -2 |
| Emre | 2 + 2 | 4 |
| Ayşe | 2 | 2 |
| Can | 2 | 2 |

Round 2. Ayşe finishes with the okey tile.

| Player | Calculation | Round points |
|---|---|---|
| Dana | 2 × 2 | 4 |
| Emre | 2 × 2 | 4 |
| Ayşe | -4 | -4 |
| Can | 2 × 2 | 4 |

Totals after two rounds:

| Player | Round 1 | Round 2 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dana | -2 | 4 | 2 |
| Emre | 4 | 4 | 8 |
| Ayşe | 2 | -4 | -2 |
| Can | 2 | 4 | 6 |

Ayşe leads with -2.

```
Total (lower leads)
Ayşe  -2 |
Dana   2 |==
Can    6 |======
Emre   8 |========
```

## Scoring in pairs (eşli)

Pair play needs exactly four players. Partners sit opposite each other: seats 1 and 3 against seats 2 and 4. Score every player as above, then add the totals of the two partners. The pair with the lower total leads.

In the example, if Dana and Ayşe are partners, their pair total is 2 + (-2) = 0. Emre and Can have 8 + 6 = 14. Dana and Ayşe lead.

## Ties

Players or pairs with equal totals share a rank. If they tie for the lowest total, both are shown as leading.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting the double penalty. A finish with okey doubles what the others pay.
- Mixing up signs. Finish values are negative on purpose.
- Missing a wrong discard. It is added on top of the round score and applies to every player, including the finisher.
- Changing values mid-game. If you change a penalty, every earlier round needs recalculating. The app does this for you.

## Questions

### What is the difference between 101 Okey and standard Okey scoring?

In 101 Okey, players score the tile sum left in their hand, with big finish and no-open values (101 and 202 by default). In standard Okey, losers all take the same small penalty and the finisher takes a small bonus.

### Can I use other values?

Yes. Many groups do. The app lets you edit every value for a match, and your defaults for new matches.

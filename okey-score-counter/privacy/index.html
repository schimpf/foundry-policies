Privacy Policy

App: Okey Score Counter (package es.schimpf.okeyscorecounter)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

Summary

Okey Score Counter does not collect, send or share your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The developer receives nothing.

What the app stores on your device

  • Player names, match names, rounds, scores and rule settings that you enter.
  • Your last chosen game and mode, and your language choice.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it. The app does not use shared storage, contacts, location, camera, microphone or any other personal data on your phone.

Network and permissions

  • The app declares no permissions. It has no internet permission and cannot connect to any server.
  • The app has no account or sign-in.
  • The app has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Backups

The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. If you change phones, your matches do not move with you.

Sharing

You can share a scoreboard as text or as an image. This only happens when you tap Share and pick another app. You choose what is included, such as the round table and real player names. For an image, the app creates a temporary file in its private cache and gives the app you pick read access to it. Once shared, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Widget

The home-screen widget shows the totals of your current match. It reads the same local data and sends nothing anywhere.

Deleting your data

  • Delete one match: open it in History and choose Delete match.
  • Delete everything: Settings, then Delete all data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your phone.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

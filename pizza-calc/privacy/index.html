Pizza Calc – Privacy Policy

App: Pizza Calc (es.schimpf.pizzacalc) · Developer: Gerrit Schimpf · Effective: 8 November 2026

Short version

Pizza Calc does not collect, send or share any personal data. It works fully offline. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your current recipe, your saved recipes and your settings (units, theme, defaults such as room and fridge temperature, oven) are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Your chosen app language is stored in the same private storage.
  • The home-screen widget reads the current recipe from that storage to show it. It does not send it anywhere.

This data never leaves your device through the app. The developer has no access to it.

Network and permissions

  • The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections.
  • It asks for no runtime permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Backup files

You can export your saved recipes to a JSON file and import such a file. You choose the location with Android's system file picker, and the app only reads or writes the single file you pick. What happens to that file afterwards (for example if you save it to a cloud folder) is up to you and the service you choose.

Android system backup

The app allows Android's own backup. If you have turned on device backup, Android may include the app's data (recipes and settings) in your device backup, for example to your Google account, and restore it on a new device. This is handled by Android and your backup provider, not by the developer, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

Third parties

No data is shared with or sold to anyone. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.

Deleting your data

Delete single recipes in the app under Recipes. To remove all app data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Exported backup files are not removed by this; delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

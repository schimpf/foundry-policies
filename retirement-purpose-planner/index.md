## About

Purpose Planner helps you give your retirement a rhythm that feels like yours. Whether you have just retired or are getting ready to, you build a simple weekly plan and keep track of what matters to you.

## Features

### What you can do

Today: see the day's plan at a glance and tick off each block as you go. Pre-retirees see the days left until their retirement date. Retirees see which day of retirement it is.

Week: build a weekly template of recurring time blocks. Each block has a title, a category (Routine, Social, Exercise, Project, Volunteering or Rest), days and times. View your week as a grid or day by day.

Projects: keep hobby, learning and home projects with milestones and a status of active, paused or done.

Volunteering: log organisation, date, hours and an optional note. See your total hours for the year and per organisation.

Weekly check-in: see how your week was spent across categories, give an optional score from 1 to 5 for how purposeful it felt, and write a note. Past check-ins stay in a list.

Starter template: begin with "First 90 days of retirement", a gentle weekly rhythm with example blocks and projects. You can change everything afterwards.

Reminders: optional notifications when a time block starts and on Sunday evening for your check-in. Each can be switched off. Notifications never show what you planned.

Widget: a home screen widget with today's plan and your countdown or day of retirement.

### Made for easy reading

Large text, high contrast, large buttons, light and dark themes that follow your phone, and support for screen readers. Works on phones and tablets.

### Your data stays with you

No account, no server, no ads and no analytics. The app has no internet permission. Everything is stored on your device. You can export a backup file, optionally protected with a password, and restore it later through the system file picker.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It builds a weekly plan from time blocks with a category, days and times. It also keeps projects with milestones, a volunteering log with a yearly total and a weekly check-in with an optional score from 1 to 5. Pre-retirees also get a countdown to their retirement date.

### What does the app not do?

It has no accounts, cloud sync, sharing, ads or analytics. It does not calculate pensions or finances.

### Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. The app has no internet permission and Android cloud backup is turned off. To keep a copy, export a backup file in Settings, optionally with a password, and restore it later with the system file picker.

### Do I need to pay for anything?

The planner, the free starter template, reminders and the Today's plan widget are free. A one-time Pro purchase through Google Play adds PDF export, extra widgets (week, projects, volunteering hours), color themes and four more starter templates. Pro is not a subscription.

### Which Android versions and languages does it support?

Android 8.0 and later, on phones and tablets. The app is in English only.

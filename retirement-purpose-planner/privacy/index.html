Privacy Policy

App: Purpose Planner (Retirement Purpose Planner), package es.schimpf.retirementpurposeplanner
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

In short

The developer collects no data about you. The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. It cannot use the internet. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

On your device only, in the app's private storage: your retirement status and date, weekly time blocks, completed blocks, projects and milestones, volunteering entries (organisation, date, hours, note), weekly check-ins (score, note), and your settings (reminders, colour theme, Pro status). This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The database is not additionally encrypted by the app. It is protected by Android's app sandbox and by your device's own storage encryption and screen lock.

Network use

The app does not declare the internet permission and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for time block reminders and the Sunday check-in reminder. You can switch each off in Settings. Reminder text is generic and does not show what you planned.
  • Run at startup: so reminders are scheduled again after a restart, time change or app update.
  • Google Play Billing: so the Pro unlock can be bought and checked.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or any account.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Payment and purchase records are processed by Google under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The app only asks the Play Store app on your device whether Pro is owned, and keeps the result on your device. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Backups, exports and the crash log

  • Backup: if you export a backup, the app writes a file to a location you choose with the system file picker. You can protect it with a password (AES-256-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2). If you lose the password, nobody can recover the file. Without a password, anyone with the file can read it. Where the file goes afterwards is up to you.
  • PDF export (Pro): works the same way and creates a file you choose.
  • Crash log: if the app crashes, it stores the error class and technical stack frames, without error messages or your entries, in private app storage. It stays on the device. You can view, save or delete it in Settings, About.

Sharing

The developer shares no data with third parties because the developer receives none.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete single entries in the app, replace everything by restoring a backup, or remove all app data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing to request from the developer.

Children

The app is for adults planning or living retirement and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.

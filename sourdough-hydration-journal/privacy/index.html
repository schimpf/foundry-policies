Privacy Policy

Sourdough Hydration Journal (package es.schimpf.sourdoughhydrationjournal)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf · Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

In short

  • The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your data.
  • The app has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.
  • The app does not request the Android internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere on its own.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Starters: names, hydration and default feeding ratio, reminder settings.
  • Feeding log entries: times, weights, ratio, notes and peak times.
  • Recipes: ingredient names and weights, notes.
  • Bake journal: recipe copy, stage times, rating, notes and timers.
  • Photos you add to a bake (Pro). A photo is copied into the app, re-encoded and reduced in size. This removes metadata such as location. The original photo is not changed.
  • App settings: units, kitchen mode and whether Pro is unlocked.

This data is never sent to the developer or to any third party by the app. Android's own cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for the app's data.

Permissions

Google Play BillingTo offer and verify the optional Pro purchase (see below).

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, calendar, or access to your files and media. Photos are chosen with the Android photo picker, and backups use the Android file picker, so the app only sees the items you choose.

Purchases

Some features need an optional in-app purchase called Pro. The purchase is handled completely by Google Play. Google processes your payment and purchase details under Google's privacy policy. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or other personal data from it. The app asks Google Play only whether Pro has been purchased on your Google account, and keeps the answer on your device.

Backups and exports

You can export a JSON backup and CSV logs (Pro) and restore a JSON backup. You choose the file location in the Android file picker. Exported files are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe. Photos are not part of backups. When you restore, the app reads only the file you pick, checks it and replaces the app's data after you confirm.

Widget and notifications

The home-screen widget shows starter names and times only. Notifications on the lock screen show a general message without your notes.

Sharing and third parties

The app does not share data with anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. The only third-party service involved is Google Play for the purchase described above.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete starters, feedings, recipes, bakes and photos inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device. Files you exported yourself stay where you saved them, and you must delete those yourself. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request or delete on a server.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. If the app ever collects data, this policy and the Google Play data safety section will be updated before that release.

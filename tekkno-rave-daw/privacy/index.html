Privacy Policy: tekkno rave daw

App: tekkno rave daw (package es.schimpf.tekknoravedaw)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 2026-11-13

Short version

The app does not collect, send or share your data. It has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. Your projects and recordings stay on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Projects, patterns, pad assignments and song layouts, in a private database of the app.
  • Samples you record with the microphone or import, in the app's private storage.
  • Settings, such as the widget name privacy switch, and a record that the paid unlock is active.

This data is not sent to the developer or to any server. The app does not allow Android cloud backup of this data.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): used only while you record a sample onto a pad. The audio is saved on your device. The app works without this permission, and you can revoke it in system settings.
  • Google Play Billing: lets the app check and start the optional unlock purchase through the Google Play Store app.

The app does not declare the internet permission and cannot connect to the internet by itself.

Files you import and export

You choose which audio files to import using the Android system file picker. The app copies the chosen file into its private storage. When you export a WAV file, you choose where it is saved. The app does not upload exports.

Purchases

The unlock is a purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes payment and account details under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the unlock is owned, and keeps the result on your device.

Home screen widget

The optional widget shows your recent project names and BPM on your home screen. You can hide the names in Settings. The widget reads data on the device only.

Sharing, analytics, advertising

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party data collection, and it shares no data with third parties.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings, then "Delete all data". This removes all projects, recordings and imported samples from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes all its data.
  • Files you exported are outside the app. Delete them with your file manager.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

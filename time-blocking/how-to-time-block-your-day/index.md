# How to Time Block Your Day, With a Worked Example

> Steps to time block a workday, a full sample schedule with durations, and how the Time Blocking app snaps and sizes blocks on a 24-hour timeline.

- Web: https://apps.schimpf.es/time-blocking/how-to-time-block-your-day/
- App: Time Blocking: Day Planner, https://apps.schimpf.es/time-blocking/index.md
## What time blocking is

Time blocking means giving every task a start time and an end time on the day's timeline. You decide the order and length of the work before the day starts, so you do not decide it again each hour.

This page gives the steps, a sample workday you can copy, and the exact rules the Time Blocking app uses when you place and resize blocks.

## Steps to time block a day

1. List the tasks for the day, including the small ones (email, calls, a break).
2. Estimate a length for each task. Round to 15 minutes. Fifteen minutes is also the shortest block in the app.
3. Place fixed events first: meetings, lunch, school pickup.
4. Place the task that needs the most focus in the time of day you work best.
5. Fill the gaps with shorter tasks. Group similar ones (all calls in one block).
6. Add the totals. If the blocks add up to more hours than you are awake, remove a task.
7. Leave one 15 or 30 minute block unplanned for overruns.

## Worked example: a 9.5 hour workday

| Start | End | Block | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 07:00 | 07:30 | Morning routine | 30m |
| 08:00 | 08:30 | Plan the day | 30m |
| 08:30 | 11:00 | Report draft | 2h 30m |
| 11:00 | 11:15 | Break | 15m |
| 11:15 | 12:00 | Email | 45m |
| 12:00 | 13:00 | Lunch | 1h |
| 13:00 | 15:00 | Project work | 2h |
| 15:00 | 15:30 | Calls | 30m |
| 15:30 | 17:00 | Admin and review | 1h 30m |

Total: 30 + 30 + 150 + 15 + 45 + 60 + 120 + 30 + 90 = 570 minutes, which is 9h 30m.

The longest block (2h 30m) sits in the morning, before email. The 15 minute break is a block of its own, so it is part of the plan.

## How to build this day in Time Blocking

- Tap an empty spot on the timeline to add a block. The start snaps down to the half hour and the block lasts 1 hour. A tap at 14:40 creates a block from 14:30 to 15:30.
- Or use the plus button, then set the start and end with the time pickers.
- Give the block a title, an optional note and one of 12 colors. Use one color per kind of work (for example blue for focus, orange for meetings) so the day reads at a glance.
- Long-press a block and drag it to move it. Drag its bottom edge to change its length.
- If you delete a block by mistake, tap Undo in the snackbar.

## How snapping works

Dragging snaps to 15 minute steps. The rules are the same everywhere in the app.

**Moving.** The new start is the dragged position rounded to the nearest 15 minutes. A block that starts at 09:00 and is dragged 22 minutes later lands at 09:15 (22 minutes is about 1.47 steps, which rounds to 1). Dragged 23 minutes, it lands at 09:30.

**Resizing.** The end time snaps to a 15 minute step, and the length follows from it. A 09:00 to 10:00 block with its bottom edge dragged 40 minutes lower would end at 10:40, which rounds to 10:45. The new length is 1h 45m.

**Limits.** The shortest block is 15 minutes. A block cannot leave the day. A 2 hour block dragged to 23:00 stops at 22:00, so it still ends at midnight.

## Overlapping blocks

Two blocks can cover the same time, for example a call during a long work block. The app draws them side by side in columns instead of on top of each other. Blocks that overlap in a chain share the same column count, so the widths stay even.

## Planning one day at a time or a whole week

On a phone, the days list shows each day with its block count, total time, a 24-hour strip and the first three blocks. Tap a day to open its timeline.

On a tablet or an unfolded foldable, the week view shows seven days side by side. Drag a block to another day to move it.

If you copy the same plan to a new day, use the day action to duplicate the whole day to another date. For tasks that repeat, see [time blocking templates and repeating blocks](../time-blocking-templates-and-repeating-blocks/).

## Keep the plan honest

- Compare the total at the end of step 6 with your real working hours. The sample day above has 9h 30m of blocks, including lunch and a break.
- Move a block with one drag when the day changes.
- After a week, look at which blocks you moved or deleted most. Give those tasks a longer estimate.

Time Blocking runs offline, keeps your plans on your device and needs Android 8.0 or newer.

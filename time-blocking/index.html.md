## About

Time Blocking is a day planner that puts your plans on a 24-hour timeline as colored blocks. Each block has a title, an optional note, a start time, an end time and one of 12 colors.

Plan a day Tap an empty spot on the timeline to add a block. The start snaps down to the half hour and the block lasts one hour. You can also use the plus button. Long-press a block and drag it to move it, or drag its bottom edge to change its length. Blocks snap to 15 minutes, the shortest block is 15 minutes, and blocks stay inside the day. Overlapping blocks are drawn side by side. If you delete a block by mistake, tap Undo.

Repeating blocks A block can repeat daily, on weekdays or on weekdays you choose, with an optional end date. When you edit, move, resize or delete a repeating block, the app asks whether the change applies to this occurrence or to all occurrences.

Templates Save a block as a template (name, length and color). When you add a block, a picker offers a blank block or one of your templates. The Templates screen lets you create, edit and delete them.

Days and weeks On a phone, the days list shows each day with its block count, total time, a 24-hour strip and the first three blocks. Tap a day to open its timeline. On a tablet or an unfolded foldable, the week view shows seven days side by side, and you can drag a block to another day.

Day actions Duplicate a block on the same day or to another date. Duplicate a whole day to another date. Clear a day, or jump to any date.

Today widget The home-screen widget lists your current and upcoming blocks, marks the running block with Now, and refreshes every 30 minutes and right after you edit in the app.

Settings Choose a light, dark or system theme. On Android 12 and newer, the app can use your wallpaper colors. Times follow your 12-hour or 24-hour setting, and the week starts on the first day of your region.

Your plans stay on your device. The app has no account and does not send your plans anywhere. It needs Android 8.0 or newer. The app is currently in English.

Time Blocking is a simple time blocking planner for people who plan one day at a time.

## Questions and answers

### What does Time Blocking do?

It shows your day as colored blocks on a 24-hour timeline. Each block has a title, an optional note, a start, an end and one of 12 colors. It supports repeating blocks, templates, a week view for tablets and a Today widget.

### What does it not do?

It has no reminders or notifications, no search, and no export, import or sync. A whole repeating series cannot be moved to another day.

### Where is my data stored?

Your plans stay on your device. The app has no account, no network access and no analytics. Cloud backup is used only when it is end-to-end encrypted.

### Do I have to pay for anything?

No. The app has no ads, no purchases and no billing code.

### Which Android versions does it support?

Android 8.0 or newer. Using your wallpaper colors needs Android 12 or newer.

### Which languages does it support?

The app is currently in English. Times follow your 12-hour or 24-hour setting, and the week starts on the first day of your region.

Privacy Policy for Time Blocking

Effective 2026-11-27. App: Time Blocking (es.schimpf.timeblocking). Developer: Gerrit Schimpf.

Short version

Time Blocking does not collect, send or share your data. Your plans stay on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your blocks: title, note, date, start time, length and color.
  • Your repeating blocks and the days you removed from them.
  • Your templates: name, length and color.
  • Your settings: theme, wallpaper colors, and whether you have seen the drag hint.
  • A small counter used to decide when to ask for a Play Store rating: how many blocks you planned, on which days, and when the app last asked.

This data lives in a local database and a local settings file inside the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it. The developer cannot read it.

Permissions

The app declares no permissions. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or notifications.

Network use

The app's code makes no network requests of its own. Two features involve other services:

  • More apps (Settings) opens https://apps.schimpf.es in your web browser when you tap it. That site is a separate page with its own handling of visitors.
  • The rating prompt is shown by Google Play after you have planned at least three blocks on at least two different days. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. The app receives no information about your answer.

Home-screen widget

The Today widget reads your blocks from the local database and shows them on your home screen. Nothing leaves the device.

Backup and transfer

Android can back up app data. Time Blocking allows cloud backup only when the backup is end-to-end encrypted. Device-to-device transfer when you set up a new phone is allowed. These backups are handled by Android and your Google account, not by the developer.

Third parties and children

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries and shares nothing with third parties. It is a general planning tool and is not aimed at children.

Deleting your data

Delete a block, template or day inside the app, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Any of these removes the data from your device. The developer holds no copy. Copies in your own Android backup are managed in your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date.

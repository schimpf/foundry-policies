## About

Time Study Stopwatch turns your phone into a time study board for the shop floor, the warehouse or the classroom.

List the work elements of a task once. Then tap one big LAP button as each element ends. The app records every element in every cycle, keeps timing while you switch apps and keeps the screen on while you work. Tapped by mistake? Undo puts the time back.

Made for industrial engineers, lean and Six Sigma practitioners, IE students and supervisors.

Included in the app: - Up to 3 studies, each with ordered work elements you can add, rename and reorder - A large lap button with haptic feedback and protection against double taps - Multiple cycles per study; a running timer survives app switches and restarts - Average, minimum, maximum and standard deviation per element and per cycle - Edit or delete any observation, with undo - Time unit in seconds, minutes or h:mm:ss - A home-screen widget showing your last study - Light and dark theme that follow your system - TalkBack support and large touch targets - Phone and tablet layouts

Time Study Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play. It unlocks: - Unlimited studies - Performance rating per observation (50 to 200 percent) - Allowance (PF&D) and standard time per cycle - CSV export to any app through the share sheet

Your data stays on your device. The app has no account, no analytics and no internet permission. Studies are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. CSV files are protected against spreadsheet formula injection.

Standard time is calculated as normal time multiplied by one plus the allowance. Normal time is the average observed time weighted by your rating.

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

You list the work elements of a task, tap one lap button as each element ends, and the app records every element in every cycle. It shows average, minimum, maximum and standard deviation per element and per cycle.

### Does it calculate standard time?

Yes. Standard time is normal time multiplied by one plus the allowance, where normal time is the average observed time weighted by your rating. It does not calculate how many observations a study needs, and it has no PDF reports or templates.

### Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no internet permission, and studies are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

### What is free and what needs a purchase?

Free: up to 3 studies, the timer and the basic statistics. Time Study Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play and adds unlimited studies, performance rating, allowance, standard time and CSV export.

### Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 or newer, on phones and tablets.

### Can I fix a wrong tap?

Yes. Undo puts the time back, and you can edit or delete any single observation afterward.

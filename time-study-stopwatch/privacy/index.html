Privacy Policy – Time Study Stopwatch

App: Time Study Stopwatch (es.schimpf.timestudystopwatch)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 13 November 2026

Short version

The app does not collect, send or share your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The developer receives nothing.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your studies: study names, work element names, allowance values, and every timed lap with its rating.
  • The state of a running timer, so timing survives app switches and restarts.
  • Your settings: time unit, and whether Pro is unlocked.

This data lives in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Network use

The app does not declare the internet permission and has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking code. It does not have accounts. It cannot send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

Purchases

Time Study Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The app only asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether you own Pro, and keeps the answer (yes or no) locally. Payment details are processed by Google under Google's privacy policy. The developer does not see them.

Permissions

  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): to check and complete the Pro purchase.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or network permissions.

CSV export

If you use Pro's CSV export, the app writes a temporary file and opens the Android share sheet. You choose the app that receives it. From then on, that app's own privacy policy applies. The temporary file is deleted by the app later.

Home-screen widget

The optional widget shows your last study. It uses the same on-device data and shows no more than the app does. Remove the widget to stop it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a study inside the app to remove it and all of its laps.
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove everything.

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing else to delete on a server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

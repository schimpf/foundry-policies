## About

Tinnitus CBT Companion puts three self-help tools on your phone: calming sounds, an 8-week lesson program and a daily check-in. It is written for adults who live with tinnitus and have no clinic program nearby.

## Features

### Sounds

White, pink and brown noise are generated on your phone. Eight nature loops sit next to them: rain, waves, stream, wind, fan, birds, fire and night. You can layer up to 3 sounds, set a volume for each one, and shape the mix with a 3-band tone control (low, mid, high). A sleep timer runs for 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes and fades out at the end. Playback continues with the screen off and has lock-screen controls. You can save mixes by name and start the last one from the home screen.

### 8-Week program

The tinnitus program has 40 short sessions, 5 per week, about 10 minutes each. Week titles include Understanding tinnitus, Calming the body, Shifting attention, Unhelpful thoughts, Better sleep, Setbacks and spikes, and Keeping it going. Your phone's text-to-speech voice reads each lesson aloud while the text stays on screen, and the current paragraph is highlighted. Each session ends with one exercise: a reflection prompt, a thought reframe, a relaxation or an attention exercise. Your written answers are saved on your phone. The lessons draw on ideas from cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT).

### Daily check-in

Rate loudness, annoyance, sleep and stress from 0 to 10 and add a note if you like. Every slider has minus and plus buttons. Trend charts cover 7 days, 30 days or the whole program, and a table view shows the same numbers. You can export the log as a CSV file to share with your doctor. A home screen widget shows whether you checked in today and draws a 7-day annoyance line.

### Reminder and help

An optional daily reminder is off until you turn it on, and it never shows your scores. If you rate loudness or annoyance 9 or 10, the app shows a link to a list of helplines. The same list is always available in Settings.

### Built for readability

Large text, large buttons, a light and a dark theme that follow your phone, layouts for phones and tablets, and screen reader labels.

### Your data

There is no account, no ads and no analytics. Check-ins, answers and mixes are stored in an encrypted database on your phone. You can make an encrypted backup file with a passphrase you choose and restore it later. The internet permission is used only for Google Play purchases and the review prompt.

### What needs a purchase

Free: week 1 of the program (5 sessions), white, pink and brown noise plus rain, one sound at a time, the daily check-in, trends, widget, backup and helplines. Full access, bought once through Google Play: weeks 2 to 8 (35 more sessions), all 8 nature sounds, layering of up to 3 sounds and the tone control.

### Important

This app is a self-help tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, cure or treat tinnitus. The lessons are general education written by the developer and have not been reviewed by a clinician. See a doctor first if your tinnitus is new, changed suddenly, is in one ear only, beats in time with your pulse, or comes with hearing loss, dizziness or pain.

## Questions and answers

### What does Tinnitus CBT Companion do?

It has white, pink and brown noise plus 8 nature sounds, an 8-week program of 40 short lessons read aloud, and a daily check-in for loudness, annoyance, sleep and stress. It also has trend charts, a home screen widget and a CSV export.

### Does it cure or treat tinnitus?

No. It is a self-help tool, not a medical device, and the lessons are written by the developer without clinician review. See a doctor first if your tinnitus is new, sudden, in one ear only, pulsing, or comes with hearing loss, dizziness or pain.

### Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in an encrypted database. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. You can make an encrypted backup file with a passphrase you choose.

### What needs a purchase?

Free: week 1 of the program, white, pink and brown noise plus rain (one sound at a time), the daily check-in, trends, widget, backup and helplines. One purchase through Google Play opens weeks 2 to 8, all 8 nature sounds, layering of up to 3 sounds and the 3-band tone control.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes. The sounds are generated on your phone, and the check-in and trends need no connection. The internet permission is used only for Google Play purchases and the review prompt.

### Which Android versions does it run on?

Android 8.0 and newer, on phones and tablets.

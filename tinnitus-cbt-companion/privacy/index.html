Privacy Policy: Tinnitus CBT Companion

Effective 2026-11-27. App: Tinnitus CBT Companion (es.schimpf.tinnituscbtcompanion). Developer: Gerrit Schimpf. Contact: apps@schimpf.es.

Short version

  • The app has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. The developer receives none of it.
  • The only network use is Google Play, for the purchase and the review prompt.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Daily check-ins: loudness, annoyance, sleep and stress scores from 0 to 10, plus an optional note.
  • Program progress and the free-text answers you write in exercises.
  • Saved sound mixes (sounds, volumes, tone settings).
  • Settings: reminder on or off and time, reading speed, whether you accepted the medical disclaimer, and whether full access is active.

The check-ins, answers and mixes are kept in a database encrypted with SQLCipher. Its key is protected by the Android Keystore. Settings are stored in a separate app file and contain no check-in or exercise text. Automatic Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app.

What leaves your phone

  • Google Play Billing. To sell and verify the one-time purchase, the app talks to Google Play. Google handles payment and your Google account. The app receives whether the purchase exists and the local price. Google's own privacy policy applies to this.
  • Google Play in-app review. The app may show the Google Play review prompt. Google controls that prompt and the app does not see your rating or text.
  • Exports you start. The CSV export and the encrypted backup file are handed to the Android share sheet. You choose where they go. The CSV file is not encrypted and is readable by anyone who gets it. The backup file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a passphrase you choose. The app never stores that passphrase, and it cannot be recovered if you forget it.
  • More apps link. Settings has a link to apps.schimpf.es. It opens in your browser only when you tap it. The link includes the tags utm_source=tinnitus-cbt-companion, utm_medium=app and utm_campaign=more_apps. It contains no personal data.

The app does not sell data, does not share data with third parties for advertising and does not build profiles.

Permissions

  • Internet and Google Play billing: only for the purchase and the review prompt.
  • Notifications: for the optional daily reminder and the playback notification. Reminder text never contains your scores.
  • Foreground service (media playback): keeps sounds and lessons playing with the screen off.
  • Receive boot completed: sets the reminder again after a restart.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or phone permissions. Lessons are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, which runs on the device.

Home screen widget

The optional widget shows whether you checked in today and a 7-day annoyance chart. Anyone who can see your unlocked home screen can see it.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of it from the phone. Restoring a backup replaces the data on the phone. Files you exported and shared are outside the app, and you delete them yourself. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing on a server to delete.

Health information

Your scores and notes are health-related. They are used only inside the app to show your trends. The app is a self-help tool and not a medical device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

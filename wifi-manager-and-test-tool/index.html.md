## About

Who is on my WiFi? Why does it feel slow? WiFi Scanner & Network Tools helps you find out, and keeps the results on your phone.

## Features

### Devices on your network

- Scan your home network and see every device that answers
- Names come from mDNS, UPnP/SSDP, NetBIOS and your router, plus a built-in vendor database
- Give devices your own names and mark them trusted or suspicious
- Devices seen for the first time are flagged, so unknown ones stand out
- Search, filter and sort the list

### Network tools

- Ping with a latency chart. If ICMP is blocked, the app uses a TCP check instead
- Traceroute
- DNS lookup: A, AAAA and reverse PTR records
- Port scan with presets or your own ports, up to 1024 per run
- Tools default to your local network. Public addresses ask for confirmation first

### Wifi info

- Network name, signal strength, band, channel, link speed and WiFi standard
- Your IP address, gateway, DNS server and subnet

### Home screen widget

- Device count and last scan time at a glance

### Pro (ONE PURCHASE IN THE APP)

Scanning, device names, trust marks, WiFi info and all tools work without a purchase. You can restore Pro on any device signed in with the same Google account.

- New-device alerts when you join WiFi and about every 15 minutes
- Device history: see when each device was online
- Export reports as CSV or PDF, saved where you choose

### Privacy

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
- Device lists stay on your phone and are excluded from cloud backup
- Nothing is uploaded. Only Google Play is contacted, to handle the Pro purchase
- Exports happen only when you ask, to a place you pick
- Settings has a Delete all data option

### Limits

Works in light and dark mode, on phones and tablets, with TalkBack. Only scan networks and hosts you are allowed to test.

- Scans IPv4 /24 networks, such as 192.168.1.0/24. No IPv6. Not made for work networks
- Android 10 and later hide MAC addresses from apps, so devices are matched by name where possible. Devices with a random MAC may show up as new when they rejoin
- Background checks run about every 15 minutes or later. Battery saver and some phone makers can delay or stop them, and a device that joins and leaves between checks can be missed
- The WiFi name needs the Location permission. This is an Android rule. The app does not use or store your location

## Questions and answers

### What does the app do?

It scans your home network and lists every device that answers, with names and vendors where it can find them. It also has ping, traceroute, DNS lookup, a port scan of up to 1024 ports per run, and a WiFi info screen with signal, band, channel and link speed.

### What does it not do?

It scans IPv4 /24 networks, such as 192.168.1.0/24, and has no IPv6 support. It is not made for work networks, and it does not block devices or change your router.

### Where does my data stay?

Device lists, names and history stay on your phone and are excluded from cloud backup. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. Only Google Play is contacted, to handle the Pro purchase, and Settings has a Delete all data option.

### What needs a purchase?

Scanning, device names, trust marks, WiFi info and all tools are free. New-device alerts, device history and CSV or PDF export are part of Pro, a single purchase in the app that you can restore with the same Google account.

### Which Android versions does it support?

Android 8 (API 26) and later. Android 10 and later hide MAC addresses from apps, so the app matches devices by name where possible, and a device with a random MAC may show up as new when it rejoins.

### Why does it ask for the Location permission?

Android requires it before an app can read the WiFi name. The app does not use or store your location, and it works without the permission except for the network name.

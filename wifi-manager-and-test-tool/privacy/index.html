Privacy Policy

App: WiFi Scanner & Network Tools (package es.schimpf.wifimanagerandtesttool)
Developer: Gerrit Schimpf
Contact: apps@schimpf.es
Effective: 13 November 2026

Summary

  • The developer collects no personal data. There is no account, no server and no analytics.
  • The app has no ads and no tracking or crash-reporting libraries.
  • Everything the app learns about your network stays on your phone.
  • The only outside service the app talks to is Google Play, to handle the Pro purchase.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Devices found by a scan: IP address, MAC address (only where Android lets the app read it), device name, vendor, your own label and trust mark, first and last seen times.
  • Networks: WiFi name (SSID) and router address (BSSID) when available, and the subnet.
  • Scan history: time, duration and device counts. Pro also keeps which devices were online, and new-device alerts.
  • Settings, such as alert options and how long history is kept.
  • A small encrypted record of whether Pro is unlocked.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your phone. The device database is protected by Android's app sandbox. It is not additionally encrypted by the app. The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer, so this data is not copied to Google or to a new phone.

Network use

  • Scanning: a scan sends pings, TCP connection attempts, mDNS, SSDP and NetBIOS requests to addresses in your own local network and reads the phone's neighbour table. The results are shown on screen and stored on the phone.
  • Tools: ping, traceroute, DNS lookup and port scan contact only the host you type in. If it is outside your local network, the app asks you to confirm first. DNS lookups go to the DNS server your phone already uses.
  • Google Play Billing: the app asks Google Play whether you own Pro and starts the purchase. Google handles payment under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment details.
  • No device data, scan results or labels are sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state, WiFi state, multicast: to scan your network and run the tools.
  • Location (fine and coarse): asked only on the WiFi screen. Android requires it to show the WiFi name. The app does not read, use or store your location.
  • Notifications: for new-device alerts (Pro). Notifications do not contain MAC addresses.
  • Billing: to buy and restore Pro through Google Play.

The app works without the location and notification permissions. You can change them at any time in Android settings.

Exports

Pro can export a CSV or PDF report. The app writes the file only when you ask, to the place you pick with Android's file picker. The file contains device names, IP addresses and MAC addresses. Once saved, it is under your control. Treat it like any private file.

Home screen widget

The widget shows the device count, the last scan time and the alert status. It reads this from the app's own data on your phone.

Sharing

The developer does not sell, share or transfer your data. The app has no advertising or analytics partners.

Retention and deletion

  • Open Settings, then Delete all data, to remove devices, labels, history and settings from the phone. Pro history is also cleaned automatically after the retention period you choose.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.
  • Your Pro purchase is kept by Google Play and can be restored. Refunds are handled by Google Play.
  • Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to delete on a server.

Security

  • Connections to Google Play use encrypted transport. The app blocks cleartext traffic.
  • Names received from your network are treated as untrusted text and are cleaned before they are shown, put in a notification or exported.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Responsible use

Only scan networks and hosts you are allowed to test.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
